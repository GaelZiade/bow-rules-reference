## Overview

**Skills** are not techniques or Release powers. They are **specialties** the character can use in everyday life or in battle: studying, picking locks, mastery of a weapon.

Anyone can try anything at a basic, non-Skill level, such as swinging a sword or studying. To be **especially good** at something, a character needs points in the matching Skill.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Points | Minimum | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Adequate** | 1 SkP | Class 8 | Basic understanding; enough to get by. |
| 2 | **Proficient** | 2 SkP | Class 5 | Pretty good; a couple of years of training. |
| 3 | **Mastered** | 3 SkP | Class 2 | An expert, almost unrivaled. |
| 4 | **Special Mastery** | **5 SkP** | **Class 1 or Rank 3** | Beyond expert; accomplishes the seemingly impossible. A lower Class can reach it at Rank 3. |

### Skill Points

- Every character starts with **15 SkP**.
- **+1 SkP per 8 completed NDE threads.** A **DP** or **DE** thread counts as **two**.
- At most **10 extra**, for **25 SkP in total**. The **Skilled** Type has a higher maximum (see *Types*).
- Levels 1–3 cost one point each. Going from 3 to 4 costs **two**, so a Skill at 4 costs 5 SkP in total.
- SkP can be **banked** for later.
- **At most two Skills at level 4** at a time, regardless of Rank. The Skilled Type may have more if it has the SkP.

### Skills back up Fighting Style

**A character cannot use anything in their Fighting Style without the Skills to back it up.** A complicated sword style needs SkP in a Swords Skill. The same applies to individual techniques and to **defenses against other races' techniques**. After pre-approval, staff will tell you if your SkP is insufficient for your Fighting Style.

### Cinematic and social Skills

Skills with **no battle influence** (singing, dancing, writing, art, and the like) **need no SkP**. They don't have to be listed in the Skills section and can stay purely In-Character.

### Other Skills

Each category allows custom **"Other"** Skills. Include them during **pre-approval**, with a note under the Skills list explaining what they do.

- **Other (Mental)** must have a use **outside battle**.
- **Other (Battle)** must be battle abilities.
- **Other (Body)** must apply to the body in some way.

The Skills template is in thread 2773.

## Battle Skills

### Weapons, Martial Arts, Street Fighting

Weapon-related non-Release techniques and fighting styles may require these Skills.

- **Weapons:** written as **"Weapon: [weapon]"**, e.g. "Weapon: Swords 3." Any weapon can be named, but one must be specified.
  - A **range** like "Firearms" instead of "Guns" covers more and is less limiting, but gives no mastery over any single weapon: only high skill in all of them.
  - A specific Skill can beat a broad one: "Knives" might overpower "Daggers."
- **Martial Arts:** works like Weapons. The art must be specified (e.g. "Martial Arts: Kung Fu"); it cannot apply generally. It also covers **Hakuda**.
- **Street Fighting:** works like Weapons but needs no specification. Pure improvisation, with basic, untrained movements.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Training | About a year | About five years | Decades; great mastery | Almost impossible movements with great success |

| Skill | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Battle Analysis**: quickly evaluate a fight, identify techniques, and respond logically | Standard | Good grasp; holds own exceptionally well | Familiar with virtually any technique | Familiar with every technique; invents unique ways to avoid them, if Stats allow |
| **Pain Tolerance**: physical pain only | Fights on through minor wounds such as cuts and second-degree burns | Third-degree burns, basic slash wounds | Ignores the pain of many attacks, even strong ones; resists severe torture, but extreme methods break it | Powers through any pain except extreme torture. The wounds still affect them. |
| **Bleeding Tolerance**: fight on through heavy bleeding | Scrapes and deep cuts | Deeper gashes to arms and torso | Losing **one** limb | Losing **two** limbs; eventually succumbs |
| **Battle Focus**: keep track of the enemy in chaos | Resists small distractions | Focuses on every aspect of the enemy, such as steps and breathing | Finds enemies through illusions, invisibility, and cloaking | Immune to weaker illusions while focused on the enemy |

- **War Strategy:** an enhanced Battle Analysis that works out the best course of action from vague clues. It benefits Support Types most. **It cannot be used in the middle of combat**, only from an objective, standstill point of view.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Poison Resist**: resistance, not immunity; some poisons specify how they interact with it | Poison takes **2×** as long to kill; normal symptoms | **3×** to kill; symptoms take 2× as long | **5×** to kill; no symptoms, but aware of being poisoned | **8×** to kill; no symptoms |

| **Acrobatics** | Athlete | Effortless Olympian | Used in battle diversely | Intertwined with fighting abilities |
| **Heat/Cold Tolerance**: you still feel it, but can cope. **One or the other, never both.** | Mildly, for a short time | Twice as long | Five times as long | Ten times as long |
| **Focused Eye**: focus perfectly on one target, ignoring everything else (may be clumsy) | Focuses fairly easily; big explosions or loud things distract | Blocks most other stimuli, such as loud noises | Perfect focus, but horribly clumsy | All of that, and **immune to illusions** while focused |
| **Stealth**: sneak, escape, disappear, stay unnoticed while not Released or charging Reiatsu | Quiet; sneaks well | Blends into shadows and crowds | Virtually unnoticeable; materializes from shadows | **Unfindable** unless they Release or charge Reiatsu |

- **Stealth cannot be used randomly.** The character must be able to hide within reason. High-ES characters need the appropriate Skills to detect a Stealth user.
- **Anti-Stealth** finds Stealth users regardless of precautions; cloaking doesn't work against it. It works when the points **match**: Anti-Stealth 3 finds Stealth 3. **Exception:** Anti-Stealth 4 only finds Stealth 4 with at least **Focused Eye 2**.

## Utility Skills

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Lock Picking** | Picks physical locks and disarms locking mechanisms, but not combination dials. More points, less time. |
| **Crafting/Forging** | Level 1: smelt weapons, craft armor. Level 2: build structures. Level 3: engineer machines. Level 4: create anything, given materials and means. **Required to craft custom weapons.** |
| **Charisma/Leadership** | Leads, inspires, gains trust. More points reach a wider range of people. Level 4 convinces even evil people or other factions to join you. |
| **Hacking** | Breaks into electronics: databases, electronic locks, devices, wiring. More points, less time. |
| **Stealing** | Pickpockets and takes objects unnoticed; escaping isn't easier (needs Stealth or Disguise). More points, less chance of being noticed. |
| **Acting** | Acts and lies convincingly. Level 1: a movie actor. Level 2: highly believable. Level 3: almost completely believable. |
| **Disguise** | Quickly and convincingly changes appearance. More points, more convincing. Same detection rules as Acting. |
| **Interrogation** | Level 1: enough for educated guesses. Level 2: more, but not everything. Level 3: almost every detail. Level 4: everything, with at least **Charisma 1**. |

**Seeing through Acting or Disguise:** **Analyzing 4** sees through **Acting 3** or lower. Against **Acting 4**, Analyzing 4 only works with at least **Focused Eye 1**.
