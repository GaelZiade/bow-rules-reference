## The Game

*Bleach: Online World* (B:OW) is a **forum roleplay** set in the universe of *Bleach*. It is an **Alternate Universe**: none of the original characters from the anime or manga exist here. Players create their own characters, who live, travel, fight, and grow across four worlds: the Soul Society, Hueco Mundo, the Human World, and Hell.

B:OW is first and foremost a **creative writing forum**. Stats and numbers determine a lot, but the game is not about who has the best Stat and Skill build.

## How Play Works

B:OW is played as **Paragraph Roleplay** (also called *Forum Roleplay*). Players write posts, made of paragraphs, inside **threads**. Each thread is a scene: a fight, an adventure, a training session, a social encounter, a job.

- A character's actions are written by their player. There is no dice roll: outcomes follow from the characters' **Stats, Skills, Class, and techniques**, applied through the battle rules (see *Battle*).
- When a thread ends, it is **reported for grading**. Staff review it and award **rewards**: Training Points, Ryo, and other gains depending on the thread type (see *Threads*).
- Rewards are spent to raise **Class**, learn **techniques**, and increase **Stats**.

## The Players

Every player may create **multiple characters**, but **each character must have its own account** on the forum. One account cannot hold several characters. Accounts can be linked through the same email.

- An account is required to post anywhere except the *Affiliates and Advertising* and *Free RP* boards. Posts made without an account are deleted.
- Do not create accounts or characters you don't intend to play.
- Register your own account; do not ask another member to register it for you.
- Account names may not be vulgar or offensive.

## The Characters

A character is defined by:

| Element | What it is | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Race** | Shinigami, Arrancar, Quincy, etc. Decides powers, organizations, and home world. | Races |
| **Release** | The race's signature power (Zanpakutō, Resurrección, Fullbring…). Unlocks at set Classes. | Races |
| **Class** | Level, from **Class 8** (weakest) to **Class 0** (strongest). | Classes & Points |
| **Stats** | Nine values bought with Stat Points (SP), plus two Auras. | Stats |
| **Skills** | Specialties rated 1–4, bought with Skill Points (SkP). | Skills |
| **Type** | A personality archetype that boosts some Stats and lowers others. | Types |
| **Rank** | Position in an organization (Rank 1–4). Strengthens Type benefits. | Ranks & Organizations |
| **Techniques** | Up to 20 learned or custom abilities. | Classes & Points, Battle |
| **Ryo** | Money, for items, NPCs, and nobility. | Classes & Points |

A character is created by posting an **application** ("app") using the race's template, which staff grade before the character can play (see *Character Creation*).

## Staff and Graders

Staff grade applications, techniques, items, upgrades, and finished threads. Approvals are posted in the thread as **1/2** and **2/2**. **Senior Graders**, experienced non-staff members, can grade standard threads and give advisory opinions on powers (see *Threads*). Staff also arbitrate conduct and disputes (see *Conduct*).

## Points at a Glance

| Point | Earned by | Spent on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TP** (Training Points) | Thread rewards | Must be converted immediately into CTP, TTP, or SP. Cannot be banked. |
| **CTP** (Character Training Points) | 1 TP → 1 CTP | Raising Class |
| **TTP** (Technique Training Points) | 1 TP → 1 TTP | Learning non-Release techniques |
| **SP** (Stat Points) | Class-ups; 2 TP → 1 SP | Stats |
| **SkP** (Skill Points) | 15 at start; +1 per 8 completed threads | Skills |
| **Ryo** | Starting funds, Job threads, Adventures, Quests, Socials | Items, NPCs, nobility |

## Reading This Book

- **Rules** are stated in the main text.
- **Advice** for building characters and roleplaying appears in separate *Advice* notes.
- Each chapter lists the forum threads it is based on. **If this book and the forum ever disagree, the forum is authoritative.**

> **Advice.** Don't hesitate to ask questions if you are confused. Staff and members are happy to help you develop your character and abilities.
