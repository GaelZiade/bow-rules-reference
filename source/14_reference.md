## About This Appendix

The forum's **Quick Reference** page collects information that comes up often, with pointers to the full rules. It is a shorthand guide, not exhaustive. The forum notes that items may be added in the future.

The page also lists two races not described in the current race rules, **Failures** and **Hason**. Their data is reproduced here as listed.

## Cutback Thresholds

If your opponent's Spiritual Pressure exceeds yours by **150 or more**, your attack damages **you** instead, on the hands, arms, or entire body depending on how and with what you attacked.

| SpPr difference | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 150 | Minor lacerations |
| 250 | Major lacerations; limbs hard to use |
| 350 | Major lacerations over the whole limb, which becomes useless |
| 500+ | Spreads to the torso near the limb; limb useless |
| 800+ | Spreads to a pair of limbs, both useless; minor lacerations on the torso |

## Grading Limits

- **Concurrent submissions:** at most **three** grading requests for anything that isn't a thread, **per player**. Regular threads are exempt.
- **Per submission:** at most **five techniques or five items**.

## Reserved Technique Slots

At most **ten** reserved techniques per character. **Technique Masters** are not restricted.
