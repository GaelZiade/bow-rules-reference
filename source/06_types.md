## Overview

A **Type** is a character's personality archetype: the kind of person they are. A Type **alters Stats**, weakening less important ones to strengthen more relevant ones. Its effects grow with the character's **Rank**, from Rank 1 (weakest benefits) to Rank 4 (strongest).

**Terms used in this chapter:**

- **Physical Stats:** Strength and Speed.
- **Spiritual Stats:** Reiatsu Strength and Spiritual Pressure.
- **Sensory Stats:** Senses, Instinct, and Energy Sensory.
- **"+50%"** means the Stat is multiplied by 1.5 (100 → 150). **"×2"** doubles it (100 → 200). **"−25%"** multiplies it by 0.75 (100 → 75).
- Percentages apply to the Stat as assigned (a Non-Typed baseline).
- **Free Skill points** granted by a Type don't count toward the SkP total and cannot raise a Skill above its normal cap.

### Changing Type

A Type change needs a **strong In-Character reason** and **at least three threads** showing the character developing toward the new Type. They can be any kind of thread, as long as they show the IC change. Non-Typed characters follow extra rules (see *Non-Typed*).

### Starting weapons

Unless a Type says otherwise, a character starts with **one weapon**: one Zanpakutō, one Doll, etc.

Types marked **"Two weapons (optional)"** may start with two. A Shinigami develops two different Shikai with different abilities that merge in Bankai; a Bount starts with two Dolls instead of one and ends with four. **No new Release technique slots** are gained: the techniques are spread across more weapons. Unless the Type says otherwise, **only Shinigami and Bounts** get this bonus.

## I. Non-Typed

### Non-Typed

An average person with nothing special about them. One of the weaker Types, mostly for beginners, but useful for developing a character toward a personality and build.

- **Advantage:** no Stat changes.
- **Free Type change.** A Non-Typed character can change Type at set points **without a CIU**, under these rules:
  - It **cannot** change into **Prodigy (v1 or v2), Technique Master, Soul Specialist,** or **Perma Release**. Other Types keep their normal requirements.
  - It needs a strong IC reason and **at least three threads** of development toward the new Type.
  - The character must first reach their primary Release or a significant development point (see the table below).
  - Types can only change **upon gaining a new Class**. Otherwise, wait for the next Class-up.
- **Disadvantage:** none.
- **Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

**Class needed before a Non-Typed character can change Type:**

| Class | Races |
| --- | --- |
| 7 | Rogues, Bounts |
| 6 | Beast-Clansmen, Hollows, Arrancar, Quincy, Akki, Nakabakki |
| 5 | Fullbringers, Mod Souls, Saika |
| 4 | Shinigami |

## II. Physical Types

Physical Types excel in melee, focusing on Strength and Speed.

### Berserker

So physically strong they need nothing else; often the minions of smarter individuals.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strength +150%. Pain Tolerance +1 (free). | Speed and Spiritual Stats −50%. Senses and ES −50%. |
| 2 | Strength +350%. Instinct +25%. Pain Tolerance +2. | Speed and Spiritual Stats −60%. Senses and ES −50%. |
| 3 | Strength +550%. Instinct +50%. Pain Tolerance +3. | Speed and Spiritual Stats −70%. Senses and ES −50%. |
| 4 | Strength +700%. Instinct +100%. Pain Tolerance +3. | Speed and Spiritual Stats −80%. Senses and ES −50%. |

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Brave

Brave heroes on the front lines: skilled and strong, but not the brightest.

| Rank | Advantage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | One Physical Stat +50%; the other +25%. |
| 2 | One Physical Stat ×2; the other +50%. |
| 3 | One Physical Stat +150%; the other +75%. |
| 4 | One Physical Stat ×3; the other ×2. |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):** Spiritual Stats halved. **Races:** all. **Weapons:** two (optional; Shinigami and Bounts only).

### Fighter Version 1

Fighters give up most Reiatsu-based abilities to rely on fighting and their weapons. Shinigami drop Kidō; Arrancar drop Cero, Bala, etc. Movement abilities such as Shunpo are unaffected. Vizards lose Kidō but keep Vizard Cero and the Vizard Mask.

| Rank | Advantage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Physical Stats +50%. |
| 2 | Physical Stats +70%. |
| 3 | Physical Stats ×2. |
| 4 | Physical Stats +150%. |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):**

- No Kidō, Hadō, Bakudō, Kaidō, Bala, Cero, or other magical spells. Step techniques are allowed.
- Fullbringers may use their basic racial Soul Command, but no techniques that expand on it.
- One Spiritual Stat −25%. **Spiritual Pressure max 150 after Type**; **HE and ES max 50**.

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Prodigy Version 1

Gifted since childhood and stronger with age, but unbalanced elsewhere.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Speed or Strength +50%; ES or Senses +50%. | SpPr or RS −33%; HE and Instinct −33%. |
| 2 | Speed or Strength ×2; ES or Senses ×2. | SpPr or RS −45%; HE and Instinct −45%. |
| 3 | Speed or Strength ×3; ES or Senses ×3. | SpPr or RS −60%; HE and Instinct −60%. |
| 4 | Speed or Strength +250%; ES or Senses +250%. | SpPr or RS −66%; HE and Instinct −66%. |

- Choices are made at pre-approval.
- **All Ranks:** can use higher-Class techniques one Class early (Class 2 techniques at Class 4 instead of Class 3).
- **Drawbacks can't be dodged** by leaving a reduced Stat at 0: put at least **25 SP per Rank** (100 at Rank 4) in each reduced Stat.
- Cannot take **Calm Mind** or **Lack of Fear** (or equivalents). Prone to mental instability and mind games.

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** two (optional; Shinigami and Bounts only).

### Runner

Fast, with the stamina to stay fast.

| Rank | Advantage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Speed +75%. "Efficient" running Speed 350; stamina costs begin at 450. |
| 2 | Speed ×2. Efficient Speed 400; stamina costs begin at 600. |
| 3 | Speed ×3. Efficient Speed 500; stamina costs begin at 700. |
| 4 | Speed +250%. Efficient Speed 600; stamina costs begin at 800. |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):** Strength halved. **Reiatsu total halved**, but not Spiritual Pressure: 200 SpPr gives 1,000 Reiatsu instead of 2,000. **Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Weapon Master

A master of weapons and arms who relies little on techniques.

| Rank | Physical Stats | Weapon transfer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +75% | Melee weapons |
| 2 | ×2 | Melee and projectile-weapon mediums |
| 3 | +150% | Melee and projectile |
| 4 | ×3 | Melee and projectile |

**Weapon transfer:**

- **Shinigami:** transfer the Zanpakutō spirit to any **melee** weapon medium. This allows two Zanpakutō with the same Release and abilities, in different mediums, both released at once, or a non-sword Zanpakutō. From Rank 2, **projectile weapons** are also allowed (a bow, a pistol; not the projectile itself).
- **Arrancar:** may transfer Resurrección to another weapon for a unique weapon, but not to activate multiple Resurrecciones.
- **Bounts:** duplicate one Doll, techniques included, for an extra identical Doll.

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):**

- Spiritual Stats −50%.
- At most **12 non-Release techniques**.
- At most **4 techniques between Shikai and Bankai**. Arrancar lose one Resurrección technique; Bounts lose three techniques spread evenly across their Dolls.
- All Release abilities **must be melee-based**.
- **ES max 100.**

**Races:** **Shinigami, Arrancar, and Bounts only.** **Weapons:** one, plus transfers.

## III. Spiritual Types

Spiritual Types excel at manipulating Reiatsu, focusing on Reiatsu Strength and Spiritual Pressure.

### Cripple Version 1

Handicapped so that physical combat is impossible, and incredibly powerful in the Reiatsu arts.

| Rank | Advantage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | RS ×2. ES ×2. |
| 2 | RS ×3. ES +150%. SpPr +50%. |
| 3 | RS +250%. ES ×3. SpPr ×2. |
| 4 | RS ×4. ES +250%. SpPr +150%. |

- **All Ranks:** may learn **3 additional non-Release techniques**.
- **Disadvantage:**
  - Must be handicapped so that physical combat is impossible: missing or non-working legs or arms, a broken neck, etc.
  - Never gets a usable physical weapon.
  - Only the **first stage of Release**. Bounts have only **one Doll**.
- **Races:** all **except Hollows and Mod Souls**. **Weapons:** one, impossible to use physically.

### Cripple Version 2

Handicapped, plus blind or deaf.

- **Advantage (all Ranks):**
  - RS ×4, SpPr ×2, ES +50%.
  - **Senses ×4 if blind**, or **×3 if deaf**.
  - Can unlock Bankai, and Bounts can have multiple Dolls.
- **Disadvantage:**
  - Cannot use a set of limbs (arms or legs), and **must** be blind or deaf.
  - No physical combat; must fight another way, such as Kidō.
  - Strength and Instinct −80%; Speed and HE −50%.
- **Races:** all. **Weapons:** one, impossible to use physically.

### Mystic

Typical "casters": ranged fighters with strong spiritual power and weak bodies.

| Rank | Advantage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | One Spiritual Stat +50%; the other +25%. |
| 2 | One Spiritual Stat ×2; the other +50%. |
| 3 | One Spiritual Stat +150%; the other +75%. |
| 4 | One Spiritual Stat ×3; the other ×2. |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):** both Physical Stats −50%. **Races:** all **except Beast-Clansmen**. **Weapons:** two (optional; Shinigami and Bounts only).

### Prodigy Version 2

A natural talent who rarely had to work hard, which can come back to bite them.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | RS or SpPr +50%; ES or HE +50%. | Strength or Speed −33%; Instinct −33%. |
| 2 | RS or SpPr ×2; ES or HE ×2. | Strength or Speed −45%; Instinct −45%. |
| 3 | RS or SpPr ×3; ES or HE ×2. | Strength or Speed −60%; Instinct −60%. |
| 4 | RS or SpPr +250%; ES or HE +250%. | Strength or Speed −66%; Instinct −66%. |

- **All Ranks:**
  - Can use higher-Class techniques one Class early.
  - Whichever of ES or HE was **not** boosted is −66%.
- **Drawbacks can't be dodged:** put at least **25 SP per Rank** (100 at Rank 4) in each reduced Stat.
- Cannot take **Calm Mind** or **Lack of Fear**. Prone to mental instability and mind games.
- **Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Spirited

A strong will and confidence that fuel spiritual power, at the cost of perception.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Spiritual Stats +25%. | Senses and Instinct −25%. |
| 2 | Spiritual Stats +50%. | Senses and Instinct −35%. |
| 3 | Spiritual Stats ×2. | Senses and Instinct −50%. |
| 4 | Spiritual Stats +150%. | Senses and Instinct −50%. |

**All Ranks:** **Healing Expertise is locked at 0**, regardless of boosts. **Races:** all, but a Spirited **Akki cannot take High-Speed Regeneration**. **Weapons:** one.

## IV. Sensory Types

Sensory Types read the world around them and turn that information into an advantage, promoting Senses, Instinct, and Energy Sensory.

### Empath

Feels others' emotions as their own and senses souls sharply. Works best one-on-one; crowds can overwhelm them.

| Rank | Advantage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Instinct and ES +50%. |
| 2 | Instinct and ES +75%. Light/Dark Aura counts as **60** around characters of **lower** Class. |
| 3 | Instinct and ES ×2. Aura counts as **80** around lower Class. |
| 4 | Instinct and ES +150%. Aura counts as **100** around characters of **their Class or lower**. Resists most emotional distress. |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):** one Physical and one Spiritual Stat −40%. Prone to cracking under pressure around many people; easily stressed in crowds. **Races:** all. **Weapons:** as Non-Typed.

### Lost Sense

Has lost a sense, but is more powerful than they let on and grows more acute with experience. Chooses **blindness or deafness** at pre-approval.

| Rank | Senses if blind | Senses if deaf | RS and SpPr if wheelchair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ×3 | ×2 | +50% |
| 2 | ×4 | ×3 | ×2 |
| 3 | ×5 | ×3 | ×2 |
| 4 | +500% (100 → 600) | +400% (100 → 500) | ×2 |

**Semi-crippled (optional, blind only):** confined to a wheelchair, with legs that don't work.

**Disadvantage:**

- Blindness or deafness.
- With the wheelchair: **no physical combat**, cannot move quickly, and **no step techniques**.

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Strategist

A thinker: more aware, intelligent, and adaptive than others. Well-paid generals are often this Type. As their Rank rises, they spend less time fighting and more time planning.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | All Sensory Stats +50%. | Physical Stats −25%. |
| 2 | All Sensory Stats ×2. | Physical Stats −25%. |
| 3 | All Sensory Stats ×3. | One Physical Stat −40%; the other −25%. |
| 4 | All Sensory Stats +350%. | One Physical Stat −45%; the other −30%. |

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

## V. Mixed/Choice Types

These Types advance several categories of Stats, by choice or by design.

### Cunning

Fights cleverly and indirectly. Reads situations and people well and has strong spiritual power, but neglects the body.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ES or Instinct +50%. RS or SpPr +25%. Analyzing +1 (free). | One Physical Stat and the non-boosted Spiritual Stat −25%. |
| 2 | ES or Instinct +75%. RS or SpPr +50%. Analyzing +2. | One Physical Stat and the non-boosted Spiritual Stat −40%.\* |
| 3 | ES or Instinct ×2. RS or SpPr ×2. Analyzing +3. | One Physical Stat and the non-boosted Spiritual Stat −50%. |
| 4 | ES or Instinct +150%. RS or SpPr ×2. Analyzing +3. | One Physical Stat and the non-boosted Spiritual Stat −50%. |

**All Ranks:** at most **6 SkP** in **Battle** and **Body** Skills, except Battle Analysis and War Strategy. **Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

\*The forum's example for Rank 2 reads "100 would be 70," which matches −30%, not −40%.

### Egotist

So focused on themselves that they shut out all outside learning. Their Release is everything to them, an obsession that often tears their soul apart.

**Advantage: more Release techniques**, which can also be made after creation.

| Race | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Shinigami | 12 Release techniques in total, split among **three Zanpakutō** |
| Rogues | Two additional Bakkōtō |
| Beast-Clansmen | **Four** advancement points per Class-up, but they **can't Class Up** past their current Class |
| Arrancar | +4 techniques per stage; two of them may run on the infinite Reiatsu |
| Fullbringers | An additional main and secondary Fullbring item |
| Bounts | 15 techniques split among up to **five Dolls** |
| Mod Souls | Two additional Mod Soul powers |
| Quincy | A second bow; twice as many properties active |
| Akki | +2 techniques per Saisei Oni level |
| Saika | Two more Dark Fullbring mutations (nine in total), with the first level of each always active |

| Rank | Release cost | Boost | Reduction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | −25% | Two Stats +25% | Two non-boosted Stats −25% |
| 2 | −50% | Two Stats +50% | Two non-boosted Stats −50% |
| 3 | −75% | Two Stats +75% | Three non-boosted Stats −50% |
| 4 | **Free** | Two Stats +100% | Three non-boosted Stats −50% |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):**

- At most **three non-Release techniques**, not counting step techniques.
- **All Releases with a duration lose two posts.**
- Reduced Stats can't be HE or ES.

**Races:** all **except Hollows and Nakabakki**. **Weapons:** generally three.

### Fighter Version 2

Like Fighter v1, Fighter v2 gives up non-Release abilities that manipulate Reiatsu (Kidō, Cero, Bala…). It does **not** lock out Reiatsu-based Releases. Shunpo and similar movement are unaffected. Vizards keep Vizard Cero and the Mask.

**Advantage (all Ranks): +1 technique per Release level**:

- Shinigami: one more for Shikai and one for Bankai.
- Arrancar: one more for Primera Etapa, and for Segunda Etapa if they have it.
- Bounts: one more per Doll.
- Akki: one more per Saisei Oni level.
- Nakabakki: one more per Saisei Oni level, plus the bonus to the Release from their non-Akki parent.
- **Rogues, Hollows, Quincy, Mod Souls, and Fullbringers get no extra abilities.**

| Rank | Stat boost | Cutback threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One Physical and one Spiritual Stat +35% | Needs a **225** SpPr difference to suffer cutback (instead of 150) |
| 2 | One Physical and one Spiritual Stat +50% | 225 |
| 3 | One Physical and one Spiritual Stat +75% | **300** |
| 4 | One Physical and one Spiritual Stat ×2 | **450** |

**Disadvantage:**

- The Physical and Spiritual Stats **not** boosted are −25%. If Strength and RS were boosted, Speed and SpPr are reduced.
- Loses Reiatsu-based abilities (Kidō, Cero, Bala, etc.). Fullbringers keep the basic racial Soul Command, but no techniques that expand on it.
- Can't make non-martial or non-weapon techniques.
- **HE locked at 0; ES max 50.**

**Races:** all **except Beast-Clansmen**. **Weapons:** two (optional; Shinigami and Bounts only, sharing Release technique slots).

### Freak

Socially disabled, easily misunderstood or hated, with completely unbalanced powers. Either a pushover or a god; creepy, insane, or terrifying no matter what, and never truly part of any society.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strength, RS, ES +25%. | Instinct −25%; SpPr −25%. |
| 2 | Strength, RS, ES +50%. | Instinct −25%; SpPr −25%. |
| 3 | Strength or RS +125%; the other ×2. ES +75%. | Instinct −33%; SpPr −33%. |
| 4 | Strength or RS +175%; the other +150%. ES +125%. | Instinct −33%; SpPr −50%. |

**All Ranks:**

- **HE stuck at 0.**
- **Release costs +50% Reiatsu.**
- **Senses max 150.**
- **Personality requirement:** prone to stupid decisions, taking hits, and acting like a freak others fear or disrespect; prone to crack under intense pressure.

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Generalist

Good at a lot of things, bad at others: picks up many small tricks instead of training one thing, and enjoys a happy medium.

**Advantage (all Ranks):**

- Unaffected by the drawbacks of high Healing Expertise.
- May skip making an **Overall** for their Release and give each technique a different theme. This is optional.

| Rank | Boosts | Cuts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One Stat +25% | One Stat (not HE or ES) −25% |
| 2 | One Stat +50%; another +25% | One Stat (not HE/ES) −50%; another (not HE/ES) −25% |
| 3 | One +75%; another +50%; a third +25% | Two Stats −50%; another −25% |
| 4 | One +100%; another +75%; a third +50%; a fourth +25% | Three Stats −50%; another −25% |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):**

- Must have **25 SP per Rank** (100 at Rank 4) in every boosted or cut Stat.
- Boosted Stats can't be cut.
- Only one of HE and ES can be cut.

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** two (optional).

### Genius

Incredibly gifted and powerful, but through **effort**, unlike a Prodigy. Little room left to improve, except stamina. **Only available at Rank 3 and 4.**

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | Physical **or** Spiritual Stats +150%. HE or ES +150%. | The non-boosted Physical/Spiritual Stat −33%; the non-boosted HE/ES −33%. |
| 4 | Physical **or** Spiritual Stats ×3. HE or ES ×3. | Same reductions. |

**Advantage (both Ranks):** **22 techniques** instead of 20.

**Fatigue:**

| Rank | Severely tired after | Fatigued after | Rest needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 12 consecutive combat posts | 6 consecutive combat posts | 2 consecutive posts |
| 4 | 14 consecutive combat posts | 8 consecutive combat posts | 3 consecutive posts |

- Past the fatigue limit, the **Primary Offensive Stat is halved**: Strength for a Physical Genius, SpPr for a Spiritual Genius. **Current Reiatsu is halved** too: 300 SpPr and 2,000/3,000 Reiatsu become 150 SpPr and 1,000/3,000.
- Resting restores the Offensive Stat, but **lost Reiatsu stays lost**.

**Combat post:** any post where the character is in a combat or defense-of-combat sequence: guarding, attacking, charging Reiatsu, acting aggressively or counter-aggressively, or controlling foreign entities (such as a Senbonzakura-style entity). A non-combat post is one where the character is doing virtually nothing.

**Other rules:**

- Put at least **25 SP per Rank** (100 at Rank 4) in each reduced Stat.
- Beast-Clansmen can't boost Spiritual Stats and must be a Physical Genius.

**Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Lunatic

Utterly insane and fights confusingly, with abilities that throw enemies off.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One Stat ×3. | All other Stats −30%. |
| 2 | One Stat ×4. Opponents treat their Battle Analysis as **1 lower** when predicting the Lunatic. | All other Stats −50%. |
| 3 | One Stat +350%. Battle Analysis treated as 2 lower. | All other Stats −60%. |
| 4 | One Stat +400%. Battle Analysis treated as 3 lower. | All other Stats −75%. |

- **All Ranks:** Release costs −50%.
- Battle Analysis can't go below 0.
- **Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Roughneck

Delinquents, thugs, hard heads: stubborn, set in their ways, solving problems with their fists. What sets them apart is that they **don't give up**.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strength or SpPr +25%; the other +50%. | One Physical or Spiritual Stat −30%. Senses or Instinct −25%. |
| 2 | Strength or SpPr +25%; the other +75%. | One Physical or Spiritual Stat −40%. Senses or Instinct −25%. |
| 3 | Strength or SpPr +50%; the other ×2. | One Physical or Spiritual Stat −50%. Either the reduced Sensory Stat goes to −50%, or the other one is also −25%. |
| 4 | Strength or SpPr +75%; the other +125%. | As Rank 3. |

**All Ranks:** **cannot acquire techniques above their current Class** until they reach that Class. **Races:** all. **Weapons:** one.

### Shadow

Assassins, spies, and saboteurs who slip in unnoticed. Dangerous with blades when confronted, but they work with limited Reiatsu and tire in prolonged fights, though less than Geniuses. Their low Spiritual Pressure helps them slip past sensors and look weak at first meeting.

**Advantage (all Ranks):**

- **Silent Release:** the first Release stage gives off **no Reiatsu signature**.
  - Races without stages: Release up to Class 4 gives none.
  - Bounts: the first Doll gives none.
  - Other stages, and Class 3+ properties or levels, give a signature as normal.
- **Precision Attack:** any primarily damaging attack, delivered at **2× OS** for that single attack. It boosts damage only, not secondary effects. The target must be **unaware** of the Shadow: for example, the Shadow stayed hidden for 2 posts, or attacks from cover or invisibility.

| Rank | Stats | Free Stealth | Precision Attack |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Speed +50%. Strength or RS +30%. One Sensory Stat +25%. | +1 | — |
| 2 | Speed ×2. Strength or RS +50%. One Sensory Stat +50%. | +2 | Once per thread |
| 3 | Speed +150%. Strength or RS +75%. One Sensory Stat +50%. | +3 | Once per thread |
| 4 | Speed ×3. Strength or RS ×2. One Sensory Stat +50%.\* | +3 | **Twice** per thread |

\*The forum's example for Rank 4 reads "100 would be 125."

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):**

- If the Release has a duration, it is **−25%** (10 posts become 8). If not, the Release and all Release-based abilities cost **+25%**.
- **Stamina posts.** A Shadow has **5 stamina posts** at every Rank. They are used up by actively engaging an enemy or actively using an ability or technique.
- **Exhaustion.** When all stamina posts are used:
  - **OS −50%.** If the OS is SpPr, this doesn't reduce the Reiatsu pool, only the power of abilities that use SpPr.
  - Step technique maximum −1. At low Classes this can remove the ability to step.
  - The Release penalties above **double**: duration halved, or costs doubled.

- **Rest posts.** To recover, take rest posts: no attacking, defending, or anything but running and hiding. Any aggressive action disqualifies the post. Rest posts are cumulative and need not be consecutive. Recovery restores OS and removes all penalties, except **Release duration already lost**.

**Races:** all **except Hollows**. **Weapons:** one, plus **15 small throwing weapons** (shuriken, kunai, senbon, throwing knives).

### Soul Specialist Version 1

Looks weak, perhaps childish, while sealed; specializes in a strong Release.

| Rank | Advantage | Sealed state: non-boosted Stats |
| --- | --- | --- |

**How it works:**

- **Sealed:** all Stats count as their **base SP values**, and the non-boosted Stats are cut as in the table.
- **On Release:** power doesn't return all at once. **Two Stats return to full value** in the first post of Release, instantly, then two more at the end of every other post.
- **When the Release ends:** Stats return to sealed values, **one Stat per post**.
- **Spiritual Pressure:** when restored to full through this mechanic, current and maximum Reiatsu rise accordingly. Regaining 200 SpPr adds 2,000 to both.
- **Fullbringers:** only the Primary Fullbring restores Stats.
- **Personality:** the sealed and Released states must be **total opposites**: lazy and manic, peaceful and hyper-violent.

**Release limits (all Ranks):**

- Races that can refresh their Release: **one Release per stage per PvP or Joint Training** (1 Shikai and 1 Bankai; 1 Primary Fullbring Level 1 and 1 Level 2; etc.).
- **Three Release durations per stage per Adventure/Quest.** The DM may waive this for especially long threads in the Special Conditions.

**Races:** all **except Beast-Clansmen, Vizards, Mod Souls, and Hollows**. **Weapons:** one.

  - **Only one Release level.**

- **Races:** all **except Beast-Clansmen, Vizards, Mod Souls, and Hollows**. **Weapons:** one.

### Exception (NPC only)

The exception to every rule: massively strong, usually an extreme villain overcome only by a sudden burst of power.

- **Advantage (all Ranks):** two Stats ×3.
- **Disadvantage:**
  - Locked to **15 SkP**.
  - Non-Release technique slots halved.
  - **Option:** reduce a non-boosted Stat by 50% to make one boosted Stat ×4 instead.
- **Restrictions:**
  - **Not available to RPCs.** A DM may use it for a plot-related, temporarily overpowered enemy.
  - **One NPC per thread.**
  - **No long-term plot NPC** may use it.
- **Races:** all, **NPC only**. **Weapons:** one.

### Experienced

An older character who has earned the title "respectable": calm, wise, and dignified, never hyper or rash. Usually appears **45–65**; Shinigami are typically **800–1,300** years old. **Ranks 3 and 4 only.**

**Rank 3:**

- One Physical and one Spiritual Stat ×2.
- **Step technique speed ×3** (a 10× step becomes 13×), at the same cost.
- Races with Release techniques: **+2 techniques** in the **lowest** Release stage. That's Shikai for Shinigami; Primera Etapa for Akki, Nakabakki, and Arrancar; one Bount Doll only.
- Races with property-based Releases: **+1 property** in the **lowest** stage:
  - Fullbringers: Fullbring Level One.
  - Quincy: a Class 4 property (Form, Firing, or Power).
  - Saika: Dark Skill Level 1.
  - Rogues: a Class 4 power.
  - Mod Souls: a Level 4 ability.

**Rank 4:**

- One Physical and one Spiritual Stat ×3.
- **+5 non-Release technique slots.**
- **Step technique speed ×5** (10× becomes 15×), at the same cost.
- Release techniques: **+2** in the lowest stage (as Rank 3), plus **+1** in the **highest** stage: Bankai for Shinigami; Segunda Etapa for Arrancar and Nakabakki; Cuarta or Quinta for Akki. For Bounts, the same Doll or another.
- Property-based Releases: **+1** in the lowest stage (as Rank 3), plus **+1** in the **highest**:
  - Fullbringers: Fullbring Level Two.
  - Quincy: a Class 2 property, anything but Soul.
  - Saika: Dark Skill Level 2.
  - Rogues: a Class 2 power.
  - Mod Souls: a Level 6 ability.

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):**

- Instinct, ES, or Senses −50%.
- The non-boosted of Strength/Speed −50%.
- **All Releases cost +50%.**

**Breakdown fatigue.** Fatigue starts when combat begins and only resets after **four consecutive posts** of rest and non-combat.

| Combat posts | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Fatigued: greatly drained, movement much harder. Boosted step speed only **twice** a post. |
| 10 | One Stat increase, chosen by the player, is lost. If SpPr, current and maximum Reiatsu drop to the new maximum (600 SpPr with 4,500 Reiatsu left becomes 300 SpPr and a 3,000 maximum). Can't fight at fullest. Boosted step speed only **once** a post. |
| 15 | The other Stat increase is lost (same Reiatsu rule). Movement ridiculously difficult. **Boosted step speed lost.** |
| 20 | The body breaks down: bones wear, crack, and snap; the body knots up. |
| 25+ | The body gradually shuts down and can no longer move or fight. |

**Races:** all **except Beast-Clansmen and Hollows**. **Weapons:** one.

## VII. Limited Types

Only a limited number of characters may hold these Types at once. The forum lists their current holders.

### Fractured

A spirit too strong for one body splits in two: one part stays in the body and the other forms a **separate spiritual body**, the **companion**. The companion holds the Release's power and a large part of the soul. Over time it develops its own personality, and the two act as inseparable partners. During **Release**, they merge into one being.

| Race | Usual form of the companion |
| --- | --- |
| Shinigami | The Zanpakutō spirit's true form |
| Arrancar | A humanoid figure |
| Fullbringers | An animal that represents their spirit (e.g. a bird for the free-spirited) |
| Vizards | A Zanpakutō manifestation, often driven by the Inner Hollow and more monstrous |

**The companion:**

- Fights alongside the user.
- **SP pool:** half the user's total SP before Type, spent on any Stat except SpPr, with **each Stat capped at 300 SP before Type**. **Type bonuses and penalties apply to it too.**
- **Spiritual Pressure:** **half the user's SpPr after Type**, locked (user 600 → companion 300). It only changes through CIUs that shuffle Stats or through **spirit-projection techniques**.
- **Projection:** a projection technique sets the companion's Stats per the technique (*Spiritual Projection* gives it the user's Stats), but for the Fractured it lasts at most **5 posts** before refreshing, whatever its normal duration.
- Has its own Reiatsu pool from its SpPr, and can use any of the user's techniques whose requirements it meets.

| Rank | Advantage | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | SpPr or RS +50%. One Physical or Sensory Stat +35%. | The other Spiritual Stat −30%. A non-boosted Stat of the same group (Physical or Sensory) −25%. |
| 2 | SpPr or RS +75%. One Physical or Sensory Stat +50%. | Other Spiritual −40%. Same-group Stat −30%. |
| 3 | SpPr or RS ×2. One Physical or Sensory Stat +75%. | Other Spiritual −50%. Same-group Stat −40%. |
| 4 | SpPr or RS ×2. One Physical or Sensory Stat ×2. | Other Spiritual −50%. Same-group Stat −50%. |

**Disadvantage (all Ranks):**

- **Origin:** the split requires a very strong IC history, or a series of IC threads building to it.
- **Slot:** the companion takes up **one non-Release technique slot**.
- **Reiatsu:**
  - While separate, the user's **maximum Reiatsu is halved** (1,000 → 500).
  - On Release, the maximum is restored and the companion's current Reiatsu is added to the user's.
  - When the Release ends and they separate, Reiatsu is **split evenly**.
- **Companion's condition:**
  - If it is **incapacitated**, the Release can't be used.
  - If it is **injured** when the Release starts, the injuries pass to the user and stay after the Release ends.
  - If it is **killed**, the Release is unusable until a significant rest period (NDE), or until two completed threads after the one where it died (DE).
- **Personality:** the two must have **distinct personalities**. The companion knows it is part of something bigger, and may **refuse** to help in some situations.

**Races:** **Shinigami, Vizards, Arrancar, Fullbringers only.** **Limit: five** at a time. **Weapons:** one.

### Kenpachi

So powerful that their soul prevents a Release from forming and denies them fine Reiatsu control, in exchange for immense power. Rare across the Three Worlds.

| Rank | Physical Stats | SpPr or RS | Flat SP added | Hold-back level | Fatigue: −20% / −50% / collapse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +75% | +50% | +50 | 350 | after 3 / +3 more / 9 posts |
| 2 | +125% | +75% | +100 | 500 | 4 / +4 / 12 |
| 3 | +175% | ×2 | +100 | 650 | 5 / +5 / 15 |
| 4 | ×3 | +150% | +200 | 700 | 6 / +6 / 18 |

**Advantage:**

- **Flat SP:** every boosted Stat gets the listed SP **added before the Type bonus**. This may exceed the normal Stat cap.
- **Overall power:** each Kenpachi gets a unique way of using their **raw energy** in combat. It is pure force or power, as rough as their spiritual power, since they lack fine control.

**Disadvantage:**

- **No Release.** A Shinigami Kenpachi can **never become a Vizard**.
- **No fine Reiatsu control:** no Kidō, Bount techniques, Gate, anything barred to the Fighter Types, or other techniques that need precision and control.
- **Fatigue**, like a Genius but different:
  - It reduces **all boosted Stats**.
  - The Kenpachi **avoids it by holding back**, keeping boosted Stats at the hold-back level.
  - At full strength, boosted Stats drop by **20%** and then **50%** after the listed number of posts. At the collapse point, the body falls apart and the Kenpachi **can't keep fighting**.
  - **Rest posts don't restore stamina.**
- **Holding back SpPr:** maximum Reiatsu drops as normal, and current Reiatsu can't exceed the new maximum. **Going all-out regains** the Reiatsu lost above the lowered maximum: 6,500/8,000 dropped to 6,000/6,000 recovers those 500.

**Races:** **Fullbringers, Bounts, Shinigami, Saika only.** **Limit: five** at a time. **Weapons:** **none**.

### Perma-Release
