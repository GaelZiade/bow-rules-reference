## Overview

Play happens in **threads**. The basic types are **Adventure**, **Battle**, **Training**, and **Social**; several others exist.

| Family | Thread types | Main reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adventure** | Adventure, Canon | TP per page, Ryo, special items |
| **Battle** | PvP, Rampage, Invasion, NPC Battle, Cinematic Combat | TP per post or paragraph |
| **Training** | Solo Training, Joint Training | TP per post (or Ryo at Class 0) |
| **Out of Combat** | Social, Shop, Job | Social bonuses, sales, Ryo |

## Thread Titles

The title carries the thread's details: a name, and optionally a difficulty, threat level, rating, and other tags. Example: **"The Fullbringers: Whirlwind of Death [R, NDE, Extreme]."** Not every detail is required, but it's wise to include as many as possible.

## Threat Level

Every thread has a **threat level**, including Socials, which are typically NDE. It sets how permanently dangerous the thread is.

| Level | Injuries | Death |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NDE** (Non-Death Enabled) | Don't carry over after the thread. You may voluntarily keep injuries that fit your character's canon. | None. A death can be hand-waved as never having happened; the character can simply be severely injured or defeated. |
| **DP** (Damage Permanent) | **Must carry over** to later threads until healed at the hospital thread or In-Character. Lost limbs, scars, damaged organs. | None. |
| **DE** (Death Enabled) | Permanent. | **Permanent and irreversible.** |

> **Advice.** DE is highly challenging and not recommended for beginners. Dungeon Masters generally won't kill you off on a whim, for the sake of the story, but they won't save you from your own mistakes either. DP threads let you feel real threat and grow from your injuries without risking death.

## Rating

The **rating** sets how graphic and violent the content can be. Higher ratings such as **"R"** may include strongly sexual or graphically violent themes: foul language, blood, gore.

- **Overly sexual or violent content must go in a spoiler or fade to black.**
- **Respect the rating set when the thread was created.**
- Staff handle rating violations. There is room for interpretation, but obvious and blatant disregard is dealt with immediately and can lead to punishment.

## Thread Template

Any thread may use the **thread template**. Modifications are fine. If used, it must be the **very first post** of the thread, separate from introductory posts.

## Adventure Threads

Adventures are the **longest** threads: an entire story with foes to fight and objectives to win by wits.

- The leader fills out the **Adventure template** as the first post, before all introductory posts, including their own.
- **Canon threads** are exactly the same as Adventures, written specifically for the site's canon.

### Thread leaders

| | Dungeon Master (DM) | Game Master (GM) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Any player leading the thread | A **forum staff member** |
| Difficulty | Up to **Hard** | Up to **Extreme** |
| Major NPCs | At most **4** major NPCs (villain, hero, support, deterrence) per thread. Minor NPCs for small fights, interactions, or information are unlimited. | Less limited |
| Usually runs | Adventures | Canon threads, plus their own Adventures |

### NPC limits by difficulty

These limits apply regardless of the leader.

| Difficulty | NPCs allowed |
| --- | --- |
| **Easy** | 2 NPCs up to Rank 2 |
| **Medium** | 2 Rank 3 + 8 Rank 2 |
| **Hard** | 1 Rank 4 + 3 Rank 3 + 12 Rank 2 |
| **Extreme** | No limit (GMs only) |

- **Hybrid difficulties** (Easy-Medium, Medium-Hard) may use the higher difficulty's limits, but the NPCs should be easier to deal with than in a full higher-difficulty thread.
- **NPCs follow the same rules as RPCs:** SP based on Class and race, and powers within what an RPC could create. A power that would be denied to an RPC is denied to an NPC.
- **Challenges must match the characters.** Don't place enemies against a player far too weak to handle them. Exceptions: purely cinematic moments, or when someone below the thread's difficulty joins.

> **Advice.** It typically isn't a good idea to join a thread whose threat level or difficulty is above your character's ability.

### Participant rewards

Rewards go only to those who **see the thread to its end**. Dropping out early forfeits them.

| Difficulty | TP per completed page | Special items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 400 | — |
| Medium | 500 | 1, depending on the thread |
| Hard | 600 | 2 |
| Extreme | 800 | 2 |

- **Ryo:** each participant gets **5× the TP earned**, but only if the thread is **completed**.
- These values are for **NDE**. **DE threads earn double.**
- **Unfinished because of the GM:** if the thread ends because the GM drops out or can't continue, participants who kept up with posts still get TP per completed page, but **no Ryo**.

### Leader rewards

The leader is rewarded only if the thread is **completed**: not cut off early, **at least three pages long**, and a full adventure, not something thrown together to reach the page count.

| Difficulty | Leader TP per completed page |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | 500 |
| Medium | 600 |
| Hard | 800 |
| Extreme | 900 |

- **Ryo:** **10× the TP earned**, if completed.
- Rewards may go to **any of the leader's characters**.
- **Unfinished because of players:** if players' inability to continue prevents the thread from moving forward, the GM earns the **average TP the participants would have earned**. If one would have earned 400 and another 600, the GM gets 500. If the GM could still reasonably move the thread forward, there's no partial reward.

## Battle Threads

Any fight between characters outside Adventures and Canons, often to prove who is better or to further a goal. Battles are one of the most popular thread types and the **primary source of TP**. Rewards are based on **posts and paragraphs**, not pages, and vary by threat level.

### Player versus Player (PvP)

A PvP is a fight between your character and another character on the site. Teams are allowed and still count as PvP.

#### Setting up a PvP

- Outline the basic conditions with the thread template, edited as needed.
- **The location is the board you post in.** To fight in Karakura Town, post in *General Karakura*: a thread made in the Soul Society can't be a fight in Karakura Town. Threads in the **Battle Zone**, which has no location of its own, must state their location.
- Each participant writes an **Introductory Post**: the character's entrance, in any context. Attacks may be made in it, but **nothing complex**.
- **List all your Stats and Skills in spoilers** at the end of the Introductory Post.

#### DE PvPs

NDE and DP fights are always available. A **DE PvP** must meet **all three** conditions below. **If any is missing, staff grade it as NDE.**

1. **Plot relevance.** More than a normal meeting: lasting effects on the characters' plots, outcomes, and psyche. Fights between members of the **same race** are usually not allowed as DE, because many organizations forbid killing within them, so most such fights count as training. Exceptions depend on the circumstances and the organization.
2. **Actual threat of death.** Spars don't count, even plot-relevant ones; usually only enemies qualify. Two Shinigami qualify only if they truly intend to kill each other. Having the potential to kill isn't enough: the intention or motivation must be there.
3. **Acknowledgement of the possibility of death.** No assumptions. By joining, you agree to let the other player kill your character if the circumstances permit.

#### PvP rewards

Each participant is rewarded for **their own posts**, which are not combined: one writing 10 posts and another 11 are graded separately.

| | NDE / DP | DE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TP per post (2–5 paragraphs) | **50** | **125** |
| TP per paragraph beyond the 5th | **10** | **25** |
| Winner's bonus | **+100** | **+300** |
| Ryo instead of TP, per paragraph | 100 | 250 |

- **Minimum post: 2 paragraphs.** Shorter posts earn nothing.
- Example: a 7-paragraph post earns 70 TP in NDE/DP and 175 TP in DE.
- This TP must be **converted immediately** to CTP or TTP, and can then possibly be converted to SP from CTP.

### Rampage Threads

An alternate form of PvP. An RPC **Hollow** goes on a rampage in the World of the Living or Rukongai, grabbing and consuming humans or souls at an alarming rate. Defenders such as Shinigami, Quincy, and Fullbringers have the biggest stake, but **any race** may join with an appropriate IC reason. The goal is to defeat or drive off the Hollow before it consumes too many souls.

- Title tag: **[Rampage]**.
- **Threat level:** forced to **DP**; it can become **DE** with the rampaging Hollow's permission.
- No set opponent: anyone with proper reasoning may join.
- **Rewards** as a normal DP or DE PvP. The Hollow's CTP from a Rampage is **not halved**.
- The Hollow must **note its consumptions** at the end of every applicable post, and should state its **Class** in the intro post in case its mini-profile is outdated.

**Who may join against the Hollow:**

| Defenders | Class requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **One** | Equal to or higher than the Hollow's Class |
| **Two** | Each at least **one Class below** the Hollow |
| **Any number** | Each at least **two Classes below** the Hollow |
| Not allowed | Anyone **two or more Classes above** the Hollow |

**Allies of the Rampager:**

- Another Hollow or sympathetic party may join to support the Rampager. The thread's Class is then the **average Class of the rampaging party, rounded down**. Members on the Rampager's side must be within **two Classes** of each other. A Class 6 Hollow joining a Class 4 Hollow's Rampage makes it count as Class 5.
- Each assistant allows **one more defender** who meets the new criteria.
- Defenders already in the thread stay, even if they now exceed the criteria. New defenders must meet the adjusted criteria.

### Invasion Threads

An alternate form of PvP. A member of any race **attacks the central headquarters** of an organization.

**Eligibility** follows the Rampage rules, using the **adjusted average Class of the attackers**. For each attacker, the defenders may be:

| Defenders | Class requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **One** | Equal to or higher than the adjusted average Class |
| **Two** | At least one Class below it |
| **Any number** | At least two Classes below it |

**Home advantage:** members of the attacked organization treat the thread's Class as **one higher**, which lets stronger members defend.

*Example from the forum:* two Class 3 Hollows and a Class 4 Hollow attack the Seireitei. Their average is (3 + 3 + 4) / 3 = 3.33, which the forum rounds to **Class 4**. Normally, for each attacker, one participant of Class 4 or higher, two of Class 5 or lower, or any number of Class 6 or lower could join. **Shinigami** defending their headquarters treat it as **Class 3**: one Shinigami of Class 3 or higher, two of Class 4 or lower, or any number of Class 5 or lower, per attacker.

| Headquarters | Defenders with home advantage |
| --- | --- |
| Soul Society: **Seireitei** | Shinigami, Vizards, Beast-Clansmen |
| Hueco Mundo: **Las Noches** | Arrancar, Hollows |
| **Silbern**: all of it | Vandenreich Quincy |
| World of the Living: **Uzaki Enterprises** | UE Employees |
| World of the Living: **Xcution Headquarters** | Xcution members |

If your organization isn't listed, private-message a moderator with its location and who benefits from defending it.

### NPC Battle Threads

These encourage players to interact with and fight **important NPCs**. They can be Training threads or PvPs, with identical mechanics either way.

- **Only staff** may control an NPC in an NPC Battle thread. Non-staff may still have and use plot NPCs however they wish.
- The staff member earns TP for the NPC even without their own character in the thread. If they have both an NPC and an RPC in it, **only the NPC** is rewarded.
- **Supplemental rewards only**, and only if the thread is **completed**. An abandoned thread is graded for the RPC, but the NPC gets nothing, which gives staff an incentive to finish.
- The NPC earns **half** the RPC's TP (RPC 100 → NPC 50). The NPC's reward can go to any of the staff member's characters.

### Cinematic Combat Threads

A battle between **two of your own characters**.

- It must be an actual battle, not just conversation or brief exchanges, and **significant to both characters**, with a lasting impact.
- **Threat level: DP or DE only.** DE follows the normal DE restrictions.
- **Minimum 25 paragraphs.**

| | DP | DE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TP to **each** character, per **combined** paragraph | 10 | 20 |

## Training Threads

Training threads make the character stronger: new techniques, better Stats, or personal development.

- **No dedicated board:** the thread goes wherever the character trains, such as their squad's barracks in the Seireitei.
- **Solo Training:** alone, practicing, studying, or meditating.
- **Joint Training:** with another character, to grow stronger together or for one to teach the other a new or reserved technique.

### What you train for

Participants may state at the **top of their first post** what they're training for: **CTP, TTP, SP, or a specific technique**.

- Graders reward accordingly. Training for TTP gives no CTP or SP, and so on.
- **If nothing is stated, the reward is normal TP**, which must be converted on receipt (see *Classes and Points*).

### Rewards

| Paragraphs in a post | TP |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer than 2 | **0** |
| 2–5 | **25** |
| Each paragraph beyond the 5th | **+5** |

Example: a 7-paragraph post earns 35 TP. **Joint Training rewards are combined** and awarded to all participants.

**Class 0 characters only** may take **100 Ryo per paragraph they write individually** instead of TP. This doesn't combine with partners' paragraphs, and partners still receive normal combined TP. Example: a Class 0 character and a partner each write 5 paragraphs; the Class 0 character gets 500 Ryo and the partner gets TP for the combined effort.

### Training a technique

Learning a technique means earning its TTP. Some techniques **require specific training**: the participant must actively work on learning and using that ability.

- Once the required TTP is earned, the technique is **Learned** and needs no further training.
- After that, TTP from **any source** may be spent on it, even if it required training.

Technique costs and teaching rules are in *Classes and Points* and *Character Creation*.

## Out of Combat Threads

These threads aren't combat-related, so they're free from restrictions such as Reiatsu costs. They are simply ways for characters to interact.

### Social Threads

Social threads cover talking, building relationships, chance encounters, parties, and the like. They have few rewards, but give room for character development: relating to others, changing personality, finding new goals.

- **A completed Social counts as one thread toward your next SkP.**
- **Open** threads can be entered by any player, regardless of affiliation, to join or ruin the event. **Closed** threads are limited to specific characters. Tag the title **[Open]** or **[Closed]**.

#### Social bonus

Relationships, positive or negative, push characters to grow, and the Social bonus models that.

- **For every 10 paragraphs** you write in a Social, you earn a one-time **+10% bonus** on the TP of the **next thread** you do with **the same character**, up to **+50%** for 50 paragraphs.
- **Each partner** gives a separate bonus. Bonuses **don't stack**: in a thread with two of those partners, use one bonus and bank the other.
- **Each Social builds its own bonus.** A second Social doesn't top up the first.
- Record bonuses in your Point Bank. They apply to any thread that awards TP (Adventure, PvP, Training), or to the **Ryo** of a Job thread.
- **Bonuses belong to the character who earned them** and can't be swapped to another of your characters.
- **Partner leaves the site or retires:** the remaining participant may use the bonus in a thread with a different character. **State this when reporting the thread**, so staff can verify.

*Example:* A writes 50 paragraphs in a Social with B and C, and gets +50% for the next thread with B and +50% for the next with C. B wrote only 35 paragraphs, so B gets +30% with each of the others. If A and B later do a second Social with 20 paragraphs each, A holds +50% and +20%, and B holds +30% and +20%; the bonuses don't merge.

### Shop Threads

Players may open their own shop, alongside Urahara's Shop, and sell any items their character can actually make.

- **Requirement: Class 3+.**
- **To open one:** send a description of the shop to the site admin by private message, or to other staff if the admin is unavailable.
- **Items:** create every item with the **Custom Item Template** (see *Items*).
- **The thread:** once approved, open it in the board where the items are sold (any location board). Staff will sticky it.
- **Proceeds** go straight to the owner and must be noted in the Point Bank after every purchase.

### Job Threads

Job threads earn Ryo through minor, tedious work: cleaning windows, washing cars, cleaning a house, anything. They might involve some fighting.

- **300 Ryo per paragraph** in the thread, to **each** participant.
- Teams must all be from the **same affiliation**.
- Posted in the **Employment Corner**.

## Grading and Reporting

### Reporting a finished thread

When a thread is finished, **report it for grading**. Staff post the grading at the end of the thread, with rewards depending on the thread type. If staff decide an incomplete thread can't be graded properly, they say so in a final post in the thread.

1. Next to the thread's **REPLY** button, click the far-right button: a **gear** or **purple oval**, depending on the theme.
2. Choose **Report Post**.
3. In the reason box, list the **thread type**, the **threat level** (NDE/DP/DE), **why** you're reporting it, and **who won**. Submit it, and it enters the staff queue.

> **Advice.** Staff differ on whether an incomplete thread can be graded. A good rule is at least **25 combined paragraphs** among all participants.

### Senior Graders

If staff haven't graded your thread **within a week**, you may ask a **Senior Grader**.

- **Who:** a non-staff member who has been on the forum **at least one year** and was officially given the position by application.
- **What they grade:** standard **combat and training** threads only. They **can't grade Canon or Adventure threads**. If they're unsure whether a thread qualifies by paragraph count, it must still be reported to staff.
- **Powers, techniques, and CIUs:** Senior Graders who have shown knowledge of balance may weigh in. Their review is **not a 1/2 approval**, only an advisory opinion, unless a **moderator backs it**. The moderator's agreement then counts as the **2/2**. If the moderator disagrees with any point of balance, it is not a full approval.
