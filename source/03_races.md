## Overview

The setting has **four worlds**: the **Soul Society**, **Hueco Mundo**, the **Human World**, and **Hell**. Each race *originates* in one of them, but that does not limit where it can go. Any character may explore any world.

Each race entry gives:

- **Release:** the race's signature power, and when its stages unlock.
- **Special rules** unique to the race.
- **Leveling path:** a table with these columns.
  - **CTP:** the total CTP needed to reach that Class.
  - **SP:** the SP gained on reaching it.
  - **Total:** the SP total without training.
- **SP caps:** without training / with training (+700) / with training and Training Tier (+200 more).

**Release durations** are counted in **posts**. When a duration ends, the Release must be **refreshed** by paying its cost again, or it ends. Some Releases cannot be refreshed.

## Soul Society Races

The Soul Society is the "heaven" of the Bleach universe. After death, souls may be sent there as **Pluses**.

- **Rukongai** has **320 districts**, 80 in each direction, numbered by distance from the center. District 1 is peaceful and lawful; District 80 is violent and crime-infested.
- The **Seireitei** is a circular fortress with **four gates**, ten days' walk apart, each guarded by a Gatekeeper. It is home to the noble families, the Shinigami, and the Academy.

Playable races here are the **Shinigami** (including **Rogues** and **Vizards**) and the **Beast-Clansmen**.

### Shinigami

Protectors of the spiritual world. Souls elevated by their spiritual power, who slay Hollows and protect souls to keep the cycle between the worlds stable.

#### Release: Zanpakutō

- **Sealed:** a sword.
- **Released:** something more powerful. It may not be a wieldable weapon, but it **always has a physical form**: an elemental or weaponized structure.
- A Zanpakutō Release **never changes the user's body**.

| Stage | Unlocks | Cost | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shikai** | Automatically at **Class 4** (counts as a Class 4 technique) | 200 Reiatsu | 10 posts, then pay again or it reverts to sealed |
| **Bankai** | By application, **Class 3 minimum** | 800 Reiatsu (at Class 3) | 10 posts |

Costs and durations improve with Class (see the leveling path).

#### Bankai

**Who may use it.** Only **Vizards** and characters Ranked **Third Seat or above**. Slots are limited:

- At most **two Third Seats** and **four Vice Captains** may have Bankai at a time.
- **Captains** and **Vizards** always have Bankai.
- An occupied slot may be **contested**. In that fight, the holder may use Bankai and the challenger may not.
- To **start with Bankai** at character creation, you must apply for a **Captain** position. Any other Rank goes through the normal Bankai application.

**Bankai application** (from Class 3, using the form in *Upgrade Your Character*):

1. **Three threads** with significant, in-depth character development pushing toward Bankai, with generous evidence in every thread that it is a serious goal. If the character is not yet of the proper Rank, these threads also count toward promotion.
2. Then, depending on the slot:
   - **Slot free:** a **solo thread** of significant development with the Zanpakutō spirit, training together. Minimum **one page**, each post at least **four paragraphs**.
   - **Slot occupied:** a **qualifying PvP** that the challenger must **win**, fought without Bankai against an opponent who may use it. It must include in-depth development with the Zanpakutō spirit, fighting together against a common "enemy."

The moderator grading the application makes the final decision. Available slots are listed in *The Soul Society Rankings*.

#### Special rules

- All Shinigami **start with Shunpo at Proficient**. Mastery must still be trained.

### Rogues

Rogue Shinigami wield **Bakkōtō**, illegal weapons made by the **Kasumi'Oji** family.

#### Release: Bakkōtō

- **No weapon-type restriction.** It can be anything.
- Usable as soon as the character reaches **Class 7**. No training is needed, unlike a Zanpakutō.
- Has **one ability and no techniques**. The ability grows in **levels** with Class, reaching several levels by Class 1.
- Every level costs **200 Reiatsu**.
- The user may always release at a **lower level** to trade power for duration.

| Bakkōtō level | 7, 6, 5 | 4, 3 | 2, 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 7 posts | 5 posts | 3 posts |

#### Life Force

Rogues have an extra Stat, **Life Force (LF)**.

- **10 LF per point of Spiritual Pressure.**
- LF can be converted **1:1 into Reiatsu Strength** to strengthen the Bakkōtō. The conversion lasts for the whole Release, even when switching levels.
- Powering the Bakkōtō does not drain LF by itself. **Every hit the Rogue takes costs 100 LF.**
- **At 0 LF, the Rogue dies.** LF is restored by resting, like Reiatsu.

#### Sacrifice

A Rogue may sacrifice **Reiatsu Strength** and **Spiritual Pressure** to strengthen **a single attack**. Example: with 100 RS, sacrificing 50 makes the attack 150 RS, and RS stays at 50 for the rest of the thread. The Bakkōtō obeys the **three-times Stat cap** (see *Stats*).

#### Zero Stage

From **Class 1**: a final attack combining **RS, SpPr, LF, and Reiatsu**. It **can bypass the three-times cap**. Afterward, LF drops to 0 and the Rogue dies shortly after.

#### Becoming a Rogue

A Shinigami who becomes a Rogue **keeps their SP** and gains every Bakkōtō level available at their Class.

#### Leveling path

| 0 | 45,000 | — | 900 | Class 0 bonuses of other Soul Reapers, except the Zero Division. Each hit costs only **50 LF**. |

**SP caps:** 900 / 1,600 / 1,800

### Vizards

Shinigami who hold an **Inner Hollow** under forced control.

#### Release

- **Shikai** and **Bankai** are **locked at 10 posts** each.
- **Bankai is required** to become a Vizard, so Vizards always have it.

#### Hollowfication: the Vizard Mask

- **Doubles all Stats.** It does not stack with other Stat boosts, except boosts from a specific Type.
- Lasts **3 posts**, with **24 hours** of rest between uses. With time and effort, it grows to **8 posts**.
- Gives **temporary infinite Reiatsu**. Costs must still be recorded. When the mask is removed or broken, all Reiatsu spent while wearing it is subtracted at once.

#### Path to Hollowfication

Vizards level exactly like Shinigami. A character starts as a Shinigami and remains one until signs of Hollowfication appear. See also *Path to Hollowfication: Guide to Becoming a Vizard* (thread 1398).

| Class | Hollowfication step |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | Meets their Inner Hollow. |
| 3 | First takeover, as the Inner Hollow makes itself known through whispers and words. Happens once. |
| 2 | One additional takeover. |
| 1 | Frequent takeovers from the slightest push, then the **Inner Hollow fight**. Losing means death; winning grants greater power and the **Vizard Mask**. |

**SP caps:** 900 / 1,600 / 1,800

### Beast-Clansmen

Descendants of feudal clans damned for a forgotten "sin." They are born into the **Beast Realm** and emerge as **anthropomorphic animals** shaped by their totem spirit. They have sharper senses, heightened instincts, and greater physical prowess at the cost of mental aptitude. As they Class up they grow more human; those who stay feral keep stronger animal powers.

#### Release: Yaseishin ("Wild Heart")

- A **body-based** Release, closer to an Arrancar's than a Shinigami's. It usually relates to the totem animal, but that is not required.
- **No sealed form.** It can be released at will.
- **No intricate uses of spiritual energy** and no high-focus abilities. It relies on raw energy and physical enhancement.
- **Unlocks** at Class 6 with a **3-post** duration and **three Class 6 techniques**.
- **Cost:** 200 Reiatsu, **+50 per Class gained**, up to **500** at Class 0.

#### Advancements

From **Class 5**, each Class-up grants one advancement. The same advancement may be taken repeatedly (e.g. six Duration Ups).

| Advancement | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Class Up** | All current Yaseishin techniques rise one Class (maximum Class 1). |
| **Technique Up** | One new Yaseishin technique, of the same Class as the others. |
| **Duration Up** | +3 posts of duration. |

Beast-Clansmen who take a Zanpakutō earn **6** advancements in total. Those who stay feral earn **7**, because at Class 0 they gain **two** advancements instead of one.

#### The Class 2 choice

- **Take a Zanpakutō.** This requires joining the **Gotei 13**.
  - Yaseishin works as before, channeled through the Zanpakutō, which must become an **item worn on the body**. If it is stolen, the powers are lost, as with any stolen Zanpakutō.
  - No Shikai or Bankai, but the character may have a Zanpakutō spirit and an Inner World.
  - The mind becomes philosophical and human.
- **Stay feral.** Grows more ferocious, with greater predatory combat intelligence, at the cost of human-like and critical thinking.
  - Without a Zanpakutō, the character can be controlled by **Central 46**.

#### Techniques

Beast-Clansmen **cannot learn Kidō** and do not start with Shinigami techniques. **Hakuda** and **Hohō** unlock gradually:

| Class | Alone (spending TTP) | With an NPC trainer (in a training thread) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before 4 | — | Up to Learned |
| 4 | Up to Learned | Up to Proficient |
| 3 | Up to Proficient | Up to Mastered |
| 2+ | Unrestricted, except techniques that require specific Ranks | — |

#### Restrictions

- Cannot take the path to Hollowfication.
- Cannot be **Kenpachi, Technique Master, Fighter v2, Weapon Master, Soul Specialist,** or **Spiritual Genius**.
- **Hollow-infusing techniques** such as the H-Pill and Bakudō #1 **kill them immediately**.
- A naturally anthropomorphic character is not automatically a Beast-Clansman. An animal from the Human World follows its own path.

#### Stat limits

| Stats | Max SP per Stat |
| --- | --- |
| Physical and Sensory | **500** |
| Spiritual | **300** |
| Energy Sensory, Healing Expertise | **250** |

#### Leveling path

| Class | CTP | SP | Total | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 0 | 480 | 480 | Barely more than animals; primal. |
| 7 | 300 | +120 | 600 | Weak; group together. |
| 6 | 500 | — | 600 | **Yaseishin** (200). |
| 5 | 1,000 | +100 | 700 | More social and human. Yaseishin 250 + advancement. |
| 4 | 1,800 | +100 | 800 | Child-like intellect. 300 + advancement. |
| 3 | 3,000 | +100 | 900 | Integrated into society. 350 + advancement. |
| 2 | 9,000 | +200 | 1,100 | Could pass for humans in their twenties. 400 + advancement. May join the Gotei 13. |

**SP caps:** 1,800 / 2,500 / 2,700

## Hueco Mundo Races

Hueco Mundo is the "purgatory": an endless desert under eternal night, home to the Hollow races.

- The **Forest of Menos** is an underground region of crystallized trees, where unaffiliated Hollows gather.
- **Las Noches** is the City of Night, a fortress under a stone dome, ruled by the Espada and home to other Arrancar.

### Hollows

Twisted souls who were not saved or purified on death.

#### No Release

Hollows have **no Release**. Instead they develop **custom techniques**, treated as **reserved** techniques and created through the normal technique process.

- All custom techniques must share **one central theme**.
- They may have as many as they like within the **20-technique** limit.
- Hollows can **only learn Bala, Ulcera, Garganta, and Cero**. **Hierro**, and any custom Cero or Bala not designed for Hollows, are off-limits.
- **Breaking the mask erases most of these techniques** (see below).

#### Leveling by consumption

Hollows earn **CTP** differently. TTP and SP are earned normally.

- **Consuming** prey gives **full CTP**.
- **Any other source gives half.** For example, 1,000 TP from training or a battle converts to only 500 CTP.
- **Exceptions at full rate:** Cinematic Battles, Rampage threads, and rewards for being DM.

**Consumption rules:**

- At least **one paragraph of combat per 50 TP** earned from a consumption.
- At the end of any post with a successful consumption, **note what was consumed and its Class**.
- **Eating above your Class:** the first Class of difference gives full TP; **each additional Class halves it**. For a Class 5 Hollow: Class 4 prey gives full TP, Class 3 half, Class 2 a quarter, and so on.
- **Consuming RPCs** (player characters) gives **double** the values below.

| NPC prey | Tier 1 (Class 8–5) | Tier 2 (Class 4–3) | Tier 3 (Class 2–1) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shinigami, Beast-Clansman | 100 | 200 | 300 |
| Hollow | 50 | 100 | 200 |
| Arrancar | 150 | 300 | 500 |
| Quincy | 100 | 150 | 200 |
| Bount | 70 | 100 | 150 |
| Human | 60 | 80 | 100 |

#### Evolution

| Class | Form |
| --- | --- |
| 8–7 | Regular Hollow |
| 6–5 | **Gillian** (Menos Grande) |
| 4–2 | **Adjuchas** (stronger at higher Class) |
| 1 | **Vasto Lorde** |

#### Breaking the mask

A Hollow becomes an Arrancar by breaking its mask:

- **Artificially**, by the **King of Las Noches**, or
- **Naturally**, by releasing its full power. This requires **more Spiritual Pressure and Reiatsu Strength than the strongest enemy it has ever eaten**.

On breaking the mask, the new Arrancar:

- **Keeps its current Class**, but is then **locked at that Class** for good. The exception is a mask broken at **Class 1**, which can still reach Class 0.
- **Loses all techniques except Bala and Cero.** The power theme is kept, and the lost techniques are replaced by the **four Resurrección techniques**.
- **Unlocks** everything tied to its Class (Garganta, Pesquisa, Hierro, etc.).
- Gains **+400 SP**. If the Hollow completed Training Tier, the bonus carries over, for a maximum of **2,300 SP** as an Arrancar.

**SP caps:** 1,000 / 1,700 / 1,900

Shinigami–Hollow hybrids created by breaking a Hollow's mask.

#### Release: Resurrección

A bodily transformation that reveals the Arrancar's true form.

- While active, it gives **"infinite" Reiatsu for non-Release techniques**. Reiatsu used is subtracted **when the Release ends**, which may cause complete exhaustion. **Release techniques** still cost Reiatsu as normal.

**High-Speed Regeneration** regrows limbs and heals bodily injuries. It is unlocked with **400 SP in Healing Expertise** before Type advantages.

#### Training exception

Arrancar can gain **1,200 SP** from training instead of 700, up to **1,900 SP**. They do **not** benefit from Training Tier.

## Human World Races

The Human World (the World of the Living, Earth) is **not inherently spiritual**. Its **Reishi** is loose and unbonded, which lets spiritual beings gather it into **Air Ground** and stand on the air. They cannot do this in other worlds. Only Quincy otherwise benefit from Reishi, by breaking its bonds to fuel their powers.

Races here: **Fullbringers**, **Quincy** (Free and Vandenreich), **Bounts**, and **Mod Souls**.

### Fullbringers

Humans whose mothers were attacked by a Hollow during pregnancy, or who lived around excessive Reiatsu.

#### Release: Fullbring

Fullbring is the **manipulation of souls**. Every object has a soul.

- The sealed Fullbring is an everyday item with no battle use (a pendant, a shirt). It **must have sentimental value**. Released, it becomes the Fullbringer's weapon.
- Once the weapon takes form, its **shape cannot change**.
- **Basic manipulation:** unlock a door, move a box, make something lighter, weaken solid materials.
- **In-depth manipulation:** controlling elements, reforming chemical compounds, disguising an item's appearance. Costs **50 Reiatsu**.
- **Limits:** you cannot manipulate someone's **personal items** unless your **Reiatsu Strength is superior** to theirs. You can **never** control the soul of someone else's **Release**, including a Zanpakutō.

**Properties.** Each property is a word or a sentence that defines the power. "Fire" adds fire; "There will be payment with each strike" might make the enemy pay a penance after each hit.

| Form | Unlocks | Cost | Duration | Properties |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary, Level One** | Weapon form at Class 5; property at Class 4 | 200 | 10 posts | **One complex property** |
| **Primary, Level Two** | Class 2 | 500 | 13 posts | **+3 properties** related to the first, bigger and more powerful (longer, more complex sentences). The weapon may become sturdier but keeps a similar size and shape. |
| **Secondary** | Class 3 (no properties); Class 1 (+2) | 300 | 8 posts | A small sidearm meant to counter other races' secondary abilities, such as Kidō or Cero. It must relate to the Primary. Its **2 properties** are only one or two words (e.g. "Frost"). |

Primary properties are basic powers, closer to casual weapons, and do not take large forms.

#### Leveling path

> **Irregular leveling.** Humans **gain no SP from Classing up**. All their SP comes from conversion.

| Class | CTP | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 0 | A normal human who can see ghosts. |
| 7 | 100 | Basic soul manipulation at no cost: break concrete with a punch or kick, pull water into the mouth, jump higher, move faster. |
| 6 | 300 | Basic elemental souls: extinguish fires, move water, halt natural winds. No cost at small scale; 50 Reiatsu at large scale. |
| 5 | 500 | **Fullbring** unlocked: weapon shape only, no properties, no cost. |
| 4 | 1,000 | First permanent **property**; weaponized like a Shikai, without techniques (200). |
| 3 | 2,400 | **Secondary Fullbring**, small-scale, no properties, summoned at no cost. |
| 2 | 5,400 | **Primary Level Two** (500). |
| 1 | 14,000 | Secondary properties (300). **Longevity:** ages slowly, like a Shinigami. |
| 0 | 45,000 | May craft Class 0 techniques; Shinigami Class 0 bonuses. Level One: 12 posts, 150. Level Two: 15 posts, 400. Secondary: no duration boost, 250. Body nearly as resilient as a Shinigami's. |

**SP caps:** 280 / 1,400 / 1,600

### Quincy

Humans who see spirits and trained to kill Hollows. Quincy arrows **destroy** a Hollow's soul instead of purifying it, which unbalances the cycle of souls. As a result, Quincy and Shinigami rarely see eye to eye.

- **Free Quincy** serve no lords beyond whoever rules where they live.
- **Vandenreich Quincy** serve the **Holy Emperor** with absolute devotion.

#### Release: Letzt Stil (Bow)

A bow made of Reiatsu.

- Fires arrows of any Class **up to the character's Class**, **maximum Class 1**.
- **Arrow cost:** 10 Reiatsu at Class 7, +10 per Class gained, up to **70**.
- **Arrow speed: 800.** Up to **five arrows** can be notched and guided.

#### Properties

Properties add effects and power. Each is **activated with a one-time cost** and stays active until switched off.

| Property | From | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Form** | Class 6 | A new shape for the bow **or** the arrows, never both with one Form (a bow becomes a sword, arrows become shields). Utility, not new powers. |
| **Firing** | Class 5 | Changes how arrows are launched: faster, curved, fired without a bow. |
| **Power** | Class 4 | An "ability" for the arrows or the Quincy: elemental damage, slipping past obstacles. |
| **Mass** | Class 3 | Number and spread of arrows (one powerful shot, or thousands of weak ones), or other "quantities" (denser arrows, a bigger bow). |
| **Heart** | Class 2 | A personal ability separate from bow and arrows, with a related theme (igniting, becoming untouchable). |
| **Soul** | Class 1 | An outward ability to interact with the environment without the bow. Generally passive. |

| Property Class | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activation cost | 100 | 150 | 200 | 250 | 400 | 500 |

**Rules for properties:**

- **Active slots:** 1 from Class 6, 2 from Class 4, 3 from Class 2. At most **3 of 6** active.
- **Repeats:** from Class 5, each new Class also lets you choose an **already unlocked** property again. A second Form, for instance, may go on the bow or the arrows.
- **Contradictions:** two contradictory properties cannot be active at once. To switch, deactivate one and pay to activate the other.
- **Arrow Class:** arrows fired must be of a Class **greater than or equal to the highest active property**. A lower-Class arrow can be used only if every active property is of that Class or lower.

> **Concentration.** Active properties reduce Reiatsu drawn in by the Quincy Cross and Spirit Fragment.
>
> | Properties active | 1 | 2 | 3 |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Regeneration | 75% | 50% | 25% |
>
> For example, with one property active, a regeneration of 300 becomes 225; with two, 150; with three, 75.
>
> The **highest number of properties active during a post** sets the rate. You cannot deactivate everything at the end of a post for full regeneration. Regeneration can never push a Quincy above maximum Reiatsu.

#### Vandenreich conversion

Members ranked **Captain Supervisor or higher** can convert willing non-Quincy into Vandenreich Quincy.

- It takes a special arrow shot **through the convert's heart**, costing **2,000 Reiatsu**.
- The convert **loses two Classes** and becomes a Quincy per the Quincy template.
- Only Fullbringers, Shinigami, Arrancar, Free Quincy, and Bounts can be converted.
- A **Free Quincy** convert suffers neither the race-change losses (see *Racial Changes*) nor the two-Class loss.

#### Vollständig

**Only Sternritter** have **Vollständig**, the Final Holy Form: an angelic form that is their ultimate power.

- On activation, sacrifice Reiatsu in steps of **500, up to 5,000**.
- Start with **6 points**, +1 per 500 Reiatsu sacrificed, up to **16**.
- Lasts **4 posts**.
- Bow properties and the Quincy Cross cannot be used normally, but the Spirit Fragment still takes in **300 Reiatsu per post**, and **one Form and one Firing property** can stay active throughout.
- Afterward, the Sternritter is **unconscious** for the rest of the thread, or a long rest.
- Enables **Vollständig-exclusive techniques**, such as *Sklaverei*.

| Benefit | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Divine Form** | 2 | An angelic form with a simple effect from the appearance change: flight, armor, arrows fired from the body. |
| **Divine Power** | 4 | A power related to the Bow Release's theme, used like a technique. |
| **Divine Soul** | 4 | A Vollständig-only Soul Property that affects the world around them for the duration. |
| **Divine Heart** | 4 | **+25% to all Stats.** |
| **Duration Extension** | 1 | +1 post (can be taken repeatedly). |

#### Weakness to Hollows

- Any contact between a Quincy and a **Hollow or Arrancar** (or extensions of them) causes **corrosive, acid-like damage** where contact was made.
- Absorbing their Reiatsu or body in any way **poisons** the Quincy, who **dies within 8 posts**.
- The only cure: another Quincy purifies them through physical contact, sacrificing **800 Reiatsu**.

### Bounts

Living beings like Quincy, but with a special component wrapped in a human soul. They **absorb human and Shinigami souls**, which is why they were once called "vampires." They are the accidental creation of a Shinigami scientist: an attempt at pseudo-Shinigami that instead made them **immortal**. Cast out of the Soul Society as dangerous, they live among humans. New Bounts are of a new generation.

- **Age limit:** no Bount may be older than **1,500 years**. Bounts were created during the time of the 33rd Captain Commander, so no "ancient" Bounts exist.

#### Release: Dolls

**Dolls** are crafted, sentient weapons that can take many shapes.

- **Sealed:** a small item of the user's choice.
- **Released** with the command **"ziege dich"** ("show yourself"), or variants in other languages. The released form follows the Doll's abilities and nature, set at creation.
- **Cost:** **400 Reiatsu** to summon. **No set duration.** It can stay released indefinitely, but this taxes the stamina of both Doll and Bount.
- Each Doll has its own personality and acts on animal instinct. If the Bount **runs out of Reiatsu or is critically wounded**, the Doll may turn on and even kill its owner.
- Each Doll has the **same total SP as its Bount**, distributed as the player chooses, and the **same Rank**. The player picks the Doll's **Type**.

**Number of Dolls.** Up to **three**, with **12 Doll techniques** in total divided evenly:

**Doll death:**

- The Bount feels intense pain and **loses 400 Reiatsu**.
- If **all three die at once**, or all die before any finishes its 5-post respawn, **the Bount dies**.

## Final Releases

### Saigo no Houmen

The **Concluding Release**: a character's final, non-permanent form and final attack. It can lead to a more powerful character, but it is **ultimately a reset: all powers are lost**.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Available to | Mod Souls, Vizards, Shinigami, Arrancar, Bounts, Quincy, Akki, Nakabakki, Hollows, Rogues |
| Not available to | **Fullbringers, Saika, Beast-Clansmen.** Characters who revert to human can later become Fullbringers. |
| Requirements | **At the SP cap** and at least **Class 1** |
| Use | **Once**, in a **single thread** |
| Creation | Submitted separately in *Create a Technique*, even after acceptance, using its own template. Pictures may be used. You must name an item the character owns as a **personal treasure**: it becomes the sealed form of a future Fullbring. |

**Stage 1**

- Reiatsu Strength and Spiritual Pressure vanish into **physical power**. All their SP, including SP from Release stages or Type, becomes new SP for other Stats and can **exceed the 400 cap**.
- **+500 SP.**
- In Reiatsu conflicts, RS and SpPr count as **infinite**: the user can bash through attacks barehanded and ignore **Hierro** and the **Cutback Effect**.
- **Step techniques cost nothing.**
- A Zanpakutō fuses with the arm. Dolls, Quincy bows, Akki chains, and the rest become a physical weapon molded into the arm. Hollows revert to a humanoid, Vasto Lorde-shaped form with a weapon in the arm.

**Stage 2** (must be activated **within 6 posts** of Stage 1)

- Lasts **1 post**.
- **All SP converts into Reiatsu Strength**, and the user takes a new form befitting the Final Release.
- The other Stats count as **1,000**, enough to find the real enemy behind illusions and reach them with a step technique.
- The attack must be a final **blast** or a **giant cut**, with a small form.

**Aftermath**

- The final form and Reiatsu fade. The user becomes a **regular human** and loses consciousness. Their age matches their apparent age.
- Akki and Nakabakki become full humans.
- Shinigami and Arrancar become humans with physical bodies visible in the World of the Living.
- Hollows return to a human state, and their holes close.
- For everyone else, it is simply a final form.

**Becoming a Fullbringer afterward**

1. Submit a new application that cites the Saigo no Houmen thread in the History, keeps all of the old History, and explains how the character learned of their Fullbring.
2. The new Fullbringer starts at **Class 5** with **300 SP**, but their cap is **2,000 SP** by Class 1, instead of 1,400.
3. Without training they reach only 700 SP at Class 1; the other 1,300 must be trained for.

### Human Metamorphosis Ritual

The Beast-Clansmen's replacement for Saigo no Houmen, a last resort for absolute crisis. The user **tears their heart from their chest**, shedding the feral form to reveal a human body of immense spiritual power.

1. **Adjustment (1 post):** all Stats are halved, but **immortality** is already in place. Injuries heal instantly and true death cannot occur.
2. **Immortality (next 3 posts):** Stats are restored, plus **1,000 SP** to distribute, which may exceed the 400 cap. The user cannot be killed and heals instantly.
3. **Final attack:** all Stats combine into **Reiatsu Strength** for one strike, after which the user collapses.
4. **Aftermath:** the user is considered dead. The human body fades and leaves a **mindless, voiceless animal** that can no longer talk or fight. This is **irreversible**. The character can stay as a social character or become an NPC through *Create an NPC*.

It is created with its own template in *Create a Technique*.

## Racial Changes

Saigo no Houmen, Vandenreich conversion, the **Redemption** option for Sinners, and the **Mask On** item are some of the ways a character's race can change. Changing race brings these losses:

| | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **SP and TTP** | All **training SP** and all **banked TTP** are lost. CTP, and Class-based SP up to the new Class, are kept. |
| **Techniques** | Techniques unavailable to the new Class and race are **lost without refund**. |
| **SkP** | Total SkP is kept. Skills follow the new Class's caps; excess SkP can be banked. |
| **Ryo and items** | Kept. Item use is limited by Class as normal. |

*"Rules for Players" gives different losses for race changes not caused by Saigo no Houmen: banked TTP and Ryo are lost; CTP, trained SP, SkP, and Training Tier benefits are kept; and unusable techniques are refunded at 75% TTP (see Conduct, Posting, and Player Rules). Check with staff which applies.*
