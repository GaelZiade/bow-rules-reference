## Overview

A **noble** has great influence through their family name, backed by money, strength, or the family's reputation. Nobles also have duties: they fight on political and social battlefields as well as physical ones, competing for power and constantly maintaining their status, with words, money, and influence as much as weapons.

| Family type | Influence | Wealth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minor family** | A minor say, like a single voter | Little |
| **Lesser noble** | Can make an observable impact | Modest |
| **Greater noble** | A large part in many of their world's major decisions | Large |

## Founding a Noble Family

Building a legacy takes time; it can't happen in a single thread or event. Anyone can belong to a **minor family**, with no real influence or power. Becoming an actual **noble** family requires the following.

### 1. A Class 1 head and an established history

- The head, or the character founding the family, must be **Class 1**. Power Tier and Training Tier Class 1 characters both qualify.
- The family must have a **clearly established history** of rising from a minor family:
  - **Power Tier:** the History section must contain a good portion of the family's history.
  - **Training Tier:** the growth must happen **In-Character**.

### 2. Rank (greater nobles)

A **greater noble** family needs at least one influential member, the head or a highly regarded member, holding a **non-Mercenary position of Rank 3 or higher**. If the position is lost, the family drops to **lesser noble** until it is met again.

### 3. Funding

| | To establish | Minimum to keep | If it drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lesser noble** | **500,000 Ryo** in the family bank | **250,000 Ryo** | **Immediately demoted**; reclaimed by reaching 500,000 again |
| **Greater noble** | **4,000,000 Ryo** | **2,000,000 Ryo** | Demoted; must earn its way back |

Greater nobles also keep a large manor, more connections, and other expenses.

### 4. Line of succession

There must **always** be a line of succession of at least one person, stated and kept updated in the family's thread. If the head retires, dies, or goes inactive, leadership passes down it.

**If there is no successor** when the head is lost, whatever their member status, the family is **demoted** until a new leader is found and the requirements are met.

> **Advice.** Always keep a successor lined up to secure the family.

### 5. Maintenance

The family must keep a distinct, proper appearance, with rules, regulations, and a reputation to uphold, and stay involved in **politics and society In-Character**: arranging marriages, meetings, and negotiations with other nobles. Political influence must be as much part of its plot as fighting.

**If this isn't done on a modestly frequent basis**, not even a paragraph or two in a thread, the head is considered unfit and staff may **demote** the family after review.

## Noble Benefits

### Influence

Major families have a lot of say in their world. In the Soul Society, the Great Noble Families' decisions shape much of what happens, such as how squads are deployed and on what missions. They may keep secrets or follow the family agenda, holding almost as much power as the Captain Commander or Central 46, through political maneuvering.

**Minor families** have no major benefits; they are a stepping stone toward higher roles.

### Family items and techniques

| | Lesser noble | Greater noble |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Family custom items** (members may buy and sell them to anyone) | Up to **10** | Up to **20** |
| **Family-exclusive techniques** (can't be taught to outsiders) | Up to **15** | Up to **35** |

**Family techniques:**

- They must be reviewed and approved by staff.
- They **don't count** toward a character's limit of **ten reserved techniques**.
- If a member who isn't the head creates one, the **head** must review it before it is submitted.

> **Advice.** Family techniques can be independent, but it's recommended to organize them into **branches of specialty**, to give each one meaning and value.

### Family techniques per character

| Character Class | 8 | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lesser noble** | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| **Greater noble** | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

These are general limits. Techniques restricted to a branch (such as the Kuchiki *Hana*) may lower a character's total.
