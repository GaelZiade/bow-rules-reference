## Overview

Fights work much like the *Bleach* canon, and many attacks and abilities are the same or nearly the same as in the show. A fight is an exchange of posts. Stats, Class, Skills, and techniques decide what is possible, and the rules in this chapter decide how they interact.

> **Advice.** Battling isn't as complicated as it sounds, and a helpful member base will guide you and answer questions. If you've never seen the show, there is a learning curve; that's what these rule sections are for.

## How a Fight Works

1. **Attack:** state an attack in your post.
2. **Defend:** in the next post, your opponent may defend. If the defense fails, the attack hits.
3. **Multiple defenses:** a target may have several layers of defense. Example: your attack breaks their weaker barrier, but in their next post they reveal an active Hierro stronger than your attack, and the second defense holds.
4. **Changed circumstances:** if circumstances change, the defender gets another chance. If their sword breaks while blocking and countering, they may **slightly** alter their actions in the next post so they make sense and allow a secondary defense. **Slightly alter means slightly alter, not change completely.**

### Auto-hit

An attack can have a **100% chance of hitting**, with no chance of defense, **only** if no defense is plausible. Example: the target is immobile, drained of Reiatsu, and has no ally to help.

### Stamina

Long, drawn-out fights tire characters. Constant exertion at high speed and power is taxing, even for characters who outlast ordinary people.

- **Show exertion:** characters must slow down, stop, pant, catch their breath. If they don't, the opponent may claim that exhaustion prevents certain actions, and depending on the circumstances that can be a plausible complaint.
- **Reiatsu depletion drains stamina.** Higher-Class techniques are more exhausting. Using technique after technique without fatigue isn't allowed; a character who does would be unable to move by the end.
- **Players must track their own character's stamina** and keep it plausible. Stamina is an interpretation, not a Stat. If a player neglects it, it can be used against them.

> **Advice.** It's smart to keep your character's stamina in mind.

### End-of-post records

At the end of every post, list whatever is relevant:

- **Stat changes** to your character or your opponent's.
- **Reiatsu** used or regained, and the amount remaining.
- *(Preferred)* descriptions of the techniques used, in spoilers, so others can read them without hunting them down.

### Hidden moves are illegal

- You **can't** dock Reiatsu now and use a technique later. A hidden move **doesn't count**.
- When you spend Reiatsu, state in the post **what it was used for, how much, and why**.
- Moves can be hidden **In-Character**. Hidden mines, for example, are allowed, but you must write where they are in that post. The opponent then plays as if their character doesn't know, unless they have a way to sense them.
- Reiatsu is **docked on activation**. **Charging doesn't dock Reiatsu.**

### Disputes

**If there is a dispute, go to a staff member.** They decide the outcome after hearing the opinions of other members, like a jury.

## Battle Politeness

**This is a rule.**

- **Don't nitpick.** Call out genuinely unreasonable details, like bending an arm into bone-breaking positions out of nowhere. Don't argue over trifles, like bending back one more inch or ducking during Shunpo. Give opponents the benefit of the doubt.
- **No one-hit KOs.** You can't kill, defeat, or neutralize an enemy RPC in one post or one physical hit. Every fight must include **more than one hit from each side** and **more than a few posts**.

### Cinematics

A **cinematic** is an action written to make the thread cooler and more theatrical: a characterization point with **no impact on the battle**, which both sides agree isn't competitive.

- If everyone agrees, a cinematic may bend mechanics.
- **Events in cinematic posts can't be used as arguments** in the thread, even if a similar move later matters.
- **Charge-up techniques don't advance during cinematics.** Seals that need a number of posts to activate don't count cinematic posts, so a battle can't be stalled for boosts.
- *(Preferred)* add an OOC disclaimer that the post is cinematic.

> **Advice.** Be polite: the thread is more enjoyable if you don't attack each other over minor details.

## Clash of Abilities

When two techniques clash, **compare their Classes**.

| Class difference | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **2 Classes** | The higher Class **wins by default**, unless the lower technique's **determining Stat** is: |
| — with **2×** the higher's determining Stat | **Draw**: they cancel out. |
| — with **3×** the higher's determining Stat | The **lower** technique wins. |
| **More than 2 Classes** | The higher Class **always wins**. |

**Class 0 exception:** only a **Class 1** technique with the **3× advantage** can break a Class 0 technique. Class 2 can't.

### Releasing at a different Class

Most techniques have a fixed Class, but some Releases can be activated **above or below** their standard Class. Shikai, for instance, is normally Class 4 for 200 Reiatsu and 10 posts.

- **Who can:** Shinigami **Zanpakutō**, **Vizard Masks**, Akki **Saisei Oni**, and Human **Fullbring**. All other races are prohibited.
- **Limits:**
  - A Release **can't exceed the user's Class**.
  - It **can't reach or pass the Class of the next stage**, nor drop to the Class of the previous one. Shikai (4) can rise to 3 but not reach Bankai's 2; Bankai can't drop to 4.
  - Normally no Release can reach **Class 0** this way. With no conflicting restrictions, it can go as low as **Class 7**.

| Change | Cost | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Up** one Class | **+150 Reiatsu** per Class | **−1 post** per Class |
| **Down** one Class | **−50 Reiatsu** per Class | **−2 posts** per Class |

- Examples: Shikai at Class 3 costs 350 and lasts 9 posts. Shikai at Class 5 costs 150 and lasts 8 posts.
- **If the cost drops to 0**, this can be done only **three times per thread**.

**Exceptions:**

- **Soul Specialist v2** (and similar Types that give up Bankai): Shikai can keep rising, up to Class 1, with higher cost and shorter duration.
- **Akki:** an Akki without Quinta Etapa can raise **Cuarta Etapa** up to Class 0. With Quinta, **Quinta** can go up to Class 0. Either way the Akki must be Class 0. This is the **only Class 0 Release exception**.

## Cutback Effect

When you hit someone whose **Spiritual Pressure exceeds yours by 150 or more**, their body pushes back and **you take the damage**. This applies to **all races except Arrancar**, who use **Reiatsu Strength** instead (see below).

| SpPr difference | Bladed attacks (lacerations) | Blunt attacks (impact) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **150** | Minor lacerations | Minor bruising |
| **250** | Major lacerations; limbs hard to use | Major bruising, possible muscle damage; limbs hard to use |
| **350** | Major lacerations over the whole limb, which becomes useless | Impact over the whole limb, possibly useless depending on the attack |
| **500+** | Spreads to the torso near the limb; limb useless | Spreads to the torso near the limb; limb **broken** |
| **800+** | Spreads to a **pair** of limbs (both arms or both legs), useless; minor lacerations on the torso | Bruises and breaks a pair of limbs; minor bruising on the torso |

Damage appears on the hands, arms, or entire body, depending on how and with what you attacked.

**What triggers it:**

- **All melee attacks:** axes, Zanpakutō, punches, kicks, and the like.
- **Not** special or Reiatsu-based attacks, such as a Reiatsu-charged blade or a Cero.
- **Releases negate Cutback** when you hit with any part of your Release.

**Getting around it:**

- **Sacrifice Reiatsu Strength** into Spiritual Pressure to increase cutting ability. Stand still and charge (you can't charge while moving), and state the amount in an **OOC paragraph** at the end of the post.
- **Arrancar:** **Reiatsu Strength** sets their cutting power, and Cutback doesn't occur if their RS is within the right numbers.
- **High Strength:** even when suffering Cutback, a character with significantly high Strength still deals blunt trauma and internal damage and can send the opponent flying or skidding.

### RS ↔ SpPr conversion

- Costs **300 Reiatsu** in either direction.
- Uses **base points only**, not points from Type boosts.
- **Raising SpPr raises maximum Reiatsu, but not current Reiatsu.** Going from 200 to 300 SpPr turns 1,600/2,000 into 1,600/3,000.
- **Lowering SpPr can cost excess Reiatsu.** Going from 300 to 200 turns 2,200/3,000 into 2,000/2,000.

See also *Stats* (once every three posts).

## Gravitational Pressure

A character of superior Class can focus their Spiritual Pressure on an inferior one and **crush** them with it.

**Requirements:**

- The user must be **Class 3 or higher**.
- There must be a gap of **at least two Classes**. A Class 1 character can use it on Class 3 or lower.

| Gap | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **2 Classes** | Pins the target to the ground; not lethal. |
| Larger gaps | Increasingly severe. |
| **5 Classes** | Can **kill instantly**. |

- **Resisting:** spend **300 Reiatsu per post**. The target still feels it, and it still hinders their abilities.
- **Range:** none in character. The only way out is to **flee and end the fight**, within reason. If you're within range to keep fighting, you feel it.
- **Class 0:** can use it with only a **one-Class** gap. Only a Class 0 can stand against another Class 0's spiritual aura, and only a Class 0 can use it on a Class 1.

### Cinematic use

Gravitational Pressure can be used **cinematically**, dialed up like a water faucet, to show off a Class difference.

- It still needs the two-Class gap.
- **No Reiatsu is needed to resist** cinematic use, and the user describes what the opponent feels.
- Cinematic use **can't be combat against one specific opponent**: it is only for flexing. **Any intent to harm** makes it full-power use, under the normal rules.

## Stat Alteration

Powers that **raise your Stats or lower others'** are **highly discouraged** and **extremely difficult to pass**. They must:

- fit the overall ability or theme of the technique or Release they belong to
- not become a dominant effect
- not be overpowered
- not conflict with existing Stat-alteration powers

Stat drains or boosts will rarely pass.

### Kinds of alteration

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Long-term** | Lasts a long period, such as several posts | Stat-alteration areas, auras, passive effects, activations (e.g. a Shikai "touch" drain), constant steps like *Hirenkyaku Constant* |
| **Short-term** | Lasts a moment: seconds or a single post | — |
| **Attack Boost** | Boosts only the technique it belongs to | Kidō incantation bonuses, Gran Rey Cero's increase |

These examples are inclusive, not limiting. **Step techniques** like Shunpo are **not** Stat alterations, even though they change Speed.

### Limits

| | Normal | Class 0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term alterations | **3** per PvP thread | **4** |
| Short-term alterations | **6** per PvP thread | **7** |

- **Outside PvP**, the same limits apply per **12 hours** instead of per thread.
- **Attack Boosts** don't count toward the short-term limit, but are limited to **one per post**.
- **Reiatsu drain isn't** Stat alteration; **Spiritual Pressure alteration is**.
- **Floor:** no reduction can lower a Stat below **25% of its base value after Type**.
- Alterations **combine additively**, but **can't bypass the three-times cap**.
- Exceeding the limits for steps or Stat alterations causes severe pain, stamina loss, and possibly the destruction of the body.

## Status Changes and Defenses

**Status effects** (illusions, poisons, auras, Stat reductions, mental manipulation, and the like) can be overpowered by default with either:

- **3× greater Reiatsu Strength**, or
- **two-Class superiority**.

**Barriers, defenses, traps, areas of effect, containment and guarding areas** follow the same **3×** rule. For defensive powers, a **3× superior offensive Stat** also breaks them.

The comparison is against the opponent's RS or SpPr, whichever is in use. **Class 0** is the only exception: no one of inferior Class can use the 3× superiority to break its effects.

## Cinematic Techniques

Non-Release techniques don't have to be combat-effective. **Cinematic techniques** are below Class 8 in power because they have no combat function.

- They **rarely cost Reiatsu** and **never inflict damage**. Example: the *Saiwan* enforces loyalty, but only if the victim speaks the same incantation and wants to be loyal.
- They are **Classed by complexity**, not power. Changing hair color might be Class 6; the *Yin Yang Seal*, which lets a Vizard blend in to the Soul Society or Hueco Mundo, much higher.
- They cost **half the TTP**, rounded to the nearest 5, and **don't count** toward the 20 non-Release techniques.

| Rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cinematic techniques | 5 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

## Illegal or Under Extreme Scrutiny

These powers are restricted to prevent "super metas": techniques that become common as players try to one-up each other in a character arms race. The goal is a fair environment in every fight.

| Power | Status | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Negation techniques** | **Illegal** | Any ability meant to negate the use of Reiatsu or other techniques. |
| **Release shutdown** | **Illegal** | Negating the use of Releases. The case may be argued before the admin, and changes may be made. |
| **Unblockables** | **Illegal** | Any effect that is absolutely unblockable. |
| **Mind reading** | **Illegal** | Direct mind reading. Cases may be argued if balanced. |
| **Alternative Stats and Resources** | High scrutiny; **mostly illegal** | Using non-OS Stats for OS-related abilities, or non-Reiatsu resources in place of Reiatsu without spending comparable Reiatsu. |
| **Sensory overload** | Extreme scrutiny | Overloading Senses or ES. If used, it goes before the admin and staff, who judge its fairness. |
| **Stat alteration** | Extreme scrutiny | Graded harshly; some cases may be argued. Staff have the final word. |
| **Area of effect** | Scrutiny | Must be written fairly. Invisible or undetectable effects, and effects with **no definite diameter limit**, are graded harshly. |
| **Super illusions** | Scrutiny; graded harshly | Illusions cast with no subjugation or strings attached. |
| **Eye techniques** | Scrutiny; graded harshly | Eye techniques with mental, emotional, or illusory effects. |
| **Forced sensory** | Heavy scrutiny | Undetectable effects that penalize low Energy Sensory. They need an "out" that low-ES characters can find, even if it's semi-difficult. |
| **Future/foresight** | Extreme scrutiny | Predicting or foreseeing the future. It pressures others into posting many possible outcomes. |
