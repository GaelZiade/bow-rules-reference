## Overview

**Stats** give numerical evidence of a character's abilities. There are nine, bought with SP, plus two **Auras** that are free.

| Group | Stats |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical** | Strength, Speed |
| **Spiritual** | Reiatsu Strength (RS), Spiritual Pressure (SpPr) |
| **Sensory** | Instinct, Senses, Energy Sensory (ES) |
| **Miscellaneous** | Healing Expertise (HE), Light Aura, Dark Aura |

### Distribution rules

- Divide SP however you like, but **your Stats must add up exactly to your SP cap before Type advantages** are applied. After Type advantages, the total may exceed the cap.
- **No single Stat may receive more than 400 SP.** The exceptions are Beast-Clansmen (see *Races*) and the Kenpachi Type, which has its own distribution rules (see *Types*).
- Stat values are **not units of measurement** and do not scale linearly: 150 Strength is not three times stronger than 50.

### What a gap means

Having a higher Stat than your opponent only makes a major difference with a **significant gap, about 100–150 points**. There are still ways for the "weaker" character to overpower the "stronger" one.

### The three-times cap

**No Stat can be boosted beyond three times its value after Type**, by any boost or technique. A Strength of 800 after Type can reach 2,400 at most. Exceptions are noted where they apply, such as step techniques for Speed.

## Offensive Stats

Three Stats are **offensive**: **Strength**, **Reiatsu Strength**, and **Spiritual Pressure**. Your **dominant** offensive Stat, the highest of the three, generally becomes your attack strength. Each Stat's entry says what it powers.

| Offensive Stat | Powers |
| --- | --- |
| **Strength** | Anything **physical**, including physically based Release forms. If it is your highest offensive Stat, it is your offense against defenses like **Hierro** and **Blut** and when your weapon's physical form clashes with an enemy's. **Not used** if the weapon has a non-physical form, such as gas. |
| **Reiatsu Strength** | **All non-Release techniques** (Kidō, Cero, Hierro, Blut), offensive and defensive. If it is your highest offensive Stat, it is also your attack strength, offensive power, and the durability of your Release weapon. Decides clashes between similar techniques. |
| **Spiritual Pressure** | **Only the Release**, Release weapons, and a Mod Soul's unique abilities. Never Reiatsu techniques or physical combat. It sets the Release's attack power if it is your highest offensive Stat, **unless** the Release is physical **and** Strength is your highest. |

### Weapons

A weapon's **durability** and **cutting power** are based on your **highest offensive Stat**. With **at least twice** the opponent weapon's offensive Stat, you break through it in one clean hit. Repeated trauma can still destroy a spirit weapon without that advantage.

### RS ↔ SpPr conversion

- Spend **300 Reiatsu**, standing still and focusing, to move points between Spiritual Pressure and Reiatsu Strength.
- Example: with 400 in both, moving 100 gives 300 SpPr and 500 RS.
- Only **once every three posts**.

## Physical Stats

### Strength

Physical strength and durability: how hard you punch, kick, and swing, and how well you take physical blows. It also helps against things like intense gravity.

| Strength | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| < 25 | **Cannot support their own body weight.** Cannot stand. |
| 50 | Average for the character's disposition; a regular human for humanoids. |
| 100 | A professional athlete of the greatest caliber. |
| 150 | Physically superhuman, like a bear. |
| 200 | Punches holes in concrete walls, craters the ground, rips chains apart. |
| 300 | Incredible jumps; hurls people through walls and over great distances; flexes to lessen physical damage. |
| 400 | Comparable to Superman: abnormally strong and highly durable, but not invincible. Weapons still hurt. |
| 600 | **Flexing guards against physical strikes**, even bladed ones, and **negates damage from any strike inferior in Strength by 150 points** to a flexed area. Doesn't work against strikes aided by Reiatsu. Weapon swings and blows create **shockwaves** just slower than Cero, visible as air distortion to anyone with **50 Senses** who sees them, and dodgeable if noticed. These swings are slightly more tiring than normal attacks. |
| 800 | Super durability: survives being hit by a car, trampled by bulls, every bone broken. Flexes broken parts back into place and survives multiple stab wounds. |
| 1,000 | Mends broken bones by crushing them together inside the body. **Punches through Reiatsu-based attacks** whose RS is inferior to this Strength, without taking damage. |

Characters of naturally stronger build are stronger than weaker-built ones with the **same** Strength. This applies only in that situation.

#### Size and minimum Strength

Larger characters need more Strength to support their own body. Values are after Type boosts.

| Height | < 8 ft | 8–15 ft | 15–20 ft | 20–30 ft | 30–40 ft | 40–50 ft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Player character** | 25 | 100 | 250 | 400 | 750 | Not allowed |
| **NPC** | 25 | 75 | 200 | 300 | 500 | 600+ |

**Player characters cannot exceed 40 feet**, regardless of Strength. NPCs get extra leeway for bosses and multi-opponent threads, and canon bosses and special NPCs may exceed 50 feet, still with high Strength.

### Speed

How quickly the character moves: running, moving an arm, blinking, even speaking.

- Speed obeys the three-times cap: 400 Speed can reach 1,200 at most.
- **Step techniques** (Shunpo, Bringer Light, etc.) raise Speed for a single step, or more depending on the technique, and **can bypass the cap**.

| Speed | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| < 25 | Can only speed-walk; dodging is almost impossible. |
| 50 | Average human. |
| 100 | Olympic track athlete. |
| 200 | Begins to blur to characters with lower Speed. |
| 300 | Matches or outruns a race car. |
| 400 | Almost disappears to the naked eye; a blur to the average human. |
| 600 | Brief flickering afterimages. |
| 800 | Multiple afterimages. Step techniques dent the ground and stir small winds. |
| 900 | Seems to disappear entirely. |
| 1,000 | Weak whirlwinds when moving. Above 1,000, basic movement creates small wind tunnels, like vacuums. |

- **Blurring:** you only blur to characters at least **150 points slower**. Two characters with 400 Speed perceive each other normally.
- **Exhaustion:** moving fast is draining. From **400 Speed** it grows increasingly exhausting: for every additional 100 points, moving at that Speed for consecutive posts drains the user by 50% of the time it usually takes. At **700 Speed**, a character can fight only a few short posts in a row while staying above 400.

> **Advice.** Unless you want a Speed-based character, putting a lot of points into Speed isn't a smart idea; 100 is a good basis. At higher Ranks, like Captain or Espada, you'll need at least 250 to match others of the same Rank. Characters faster than 1,000 will likely want to hold back.

## Spiritual Stats

### Reiatsu Strength

**Without at least 25 Reiatsu Strength, a character cannot use any spiritual abilities**, Release or non-Release.

- The higher it is, the bigger and stronger your techniques.
- When two similar techniques clash (e.g. Kidō vs. Cero), the higher RS wins, except across **Class differences** (see *Clash of Abilities* in *Battle*). **No RS compensates for a difference of 3+ Classes.**
- When attacking someone's Release, compare against your opponent's **largest offensive Stat**.
- RS obeys the three-times cap: 1,000 RS can reach 3,000 at most.

| Reiatsu Strength | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| ≥ opponent's | Cuts straight through their defenses, even muscular ones. |
| 600 | Can release Reiatsu from the body to menace others, or in small bursts as blinding flashes. |
| 800 | Exerts extremely intimidating pressure. |
| 1,000 | **Flexing Reiatsu** guards against Reiatsu attacks, but not physical ones: it negates damage from explosive or Reiatsu-based attacks whose RS is **at least 100 lower**. Exhausting, and hard to do over the whole body. **Technique Masters cannot use this defense.** |
| 1,200 | Immune to **Senka**'s primary effect, which destroys the soul chain to erase powers. |

**Class 0 masking:** Class 0 characters can mask their Reiatsu so perfectly that **no one of inferior Class can detect it**. Their presence can still be sensed. This is mostly cinematic in fights but useful in Adventures, Quests, and Canons.

### Spiritual Pressure

Spiritual Pressure measures **Reiatsu**, the basic unit of energy that almost every technique and Release costs.

- **1 SpPr = 10 Reiatsu.** 100 SpPr gives 1,000 Reiatsu.
- **0 SpPr** means no spiritual abilities at all, Release included.
- It governs the **Cutback Effect** (see *Battle*).
- As an offensive Stat, it applies **only** to the Release (see *Offensive Stats*).

## Sensory Stats

### Instinct

Mental speed in battle and a "sixth sense": noticing someone sneaking up, sensing that something is wrong, reacting to danger. Avoiding or defending still depends on **Speed** and **Strength**; thinking fast doesn't mean the body can move fast enough.

| Instinct | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| < 25 | Significantly below average; cannot avoid attacks unless they come straight on, and even then struggles. |
| 50 | Average human. |
| 100 | High-level Olympian. |
| 150 | Savage animal. |
| 200 | Empathizes with and partly tames wild animals. |
| 300 | Processes all non-visual information flawlessly; reads attack patterns easily. |
| 450 | **"Super deduction":** understands how something works after seeing it once. |
| 600 | Discerns all of an opponent's strengths and weaknesses and how to exploit them. Tames any wild animal with a simple command. |

> **Advice.** Most characters don't need more than 150 Instinct, but those who go higher are rewarded.

### Senses

Sight, smell, hearing, touch, and taste in one Stat. **Senses** gather information, **Instinct** interprets it, and **Speed** decides whether you can react: high Senses won't help against someone significantly faster.

| Senses | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| < 25 | Severely at risk of being disabled: awful eyesight, hardly any smell, faulty nerves. |
| 50 | Average human. |
| 100 | Wild animal. |
| 300 | **Super senses:** hears twitches, sees minor alterations and perspiration. |
| 450 | Detects every detail without fail, including poisons or toxins in an opponent. Hears and distinguishes heartbeats. Virtually impossible to sneak up on. |
| 600 | **No blind spots:** sees 360° just by moving the eyes. |
| 900 | Detects minor changes anywhere in the world. |
| 1,000 | Sees anything down to its finest detail and can cite all information about it. **Temporarily copies physical actions.** |

**Overload:** very high Senses can be overwhelmed. At **300+**, a dog whistle or a blinding flash hurts far more. At **600+**, the character often has to "subdue" some senses to concentrate.

> **Advice.** Most characters don't typically need more than 150 Senses, but anyone can go higher.

### Energy Sensory

Detects **Reiatsu**. Even at **0**, a character senses the difference in power between themselves and others, and can tell the race of someone who **focuses** their Reiatsu on them.

| Energy Sensory | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| < 25 | Detects traces of Reiatsu without it being focused on you; nothing extraordinary. |
| 50 | Average. Senses Reiatsu in the area and how strong people are, flaring Reiatsu, and attacks being charged. |
| 100 | Individual signatures within **half a mile**, and their race. Recognizes familiar signatures. |
| 150 | Exact strength, race, and basic personality traits. |
| 200 | **Detects masked Reiatsu.** |
| 400 | Reads basic nuances of a soul: characteristics, emotions, split-second thoughts, glimpses of history. |
| 500 | Understands what drives others. With **100 Light Aura**, knows what to say to win someone over; with **100 Dark Aura**, how to traumatize them. |
| 600 | Basic communication with spirits in another's soul, such as a **Zanpakutō spirit**, if at least the same Class as its wielder. |
| 800 | Reads deep into a soul and knows **what its Release does**. |
| 1,000 | With **100 Light or Dark Aura** and **Class superiority**, convinces a Zanpakutō spirit to **betray its master** and negate its Release. Doesn't work on **Class 0**; the victim can overcome it after **five posts** of internal dialogue. |

**Masking Reiatsu:** anyone can mask their Reiatsu while **not Released or charging** an attack, which makes them hard to detect below **200 ES**. Beyond that, hiding requires the **Stealth** Skill, and finding a Stealth user requires **Anti-Stealth** and **Focused Eye** (see *Skills*).

## Miscellaneous Stats

### Light Aura and Dark Aura

- Every character gets **100 free points** to divide between the two Auras. They **don't count toward the SP total** and never increase or decrease.
- The Aura is the character's inherent personality and the feel of their Reiatsu: how good or evil it seems. Neither has a major drawback.
- **50/50** is neutral. Seeming good or evil requires a **significant** difference. A traditional "good guy" has no Dark Aura; a traditional "bad guy" has no Light Aura.

| Aura | Light Aura | Dark Aura |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Spreads a good, charismatic, cheerful feeling; wards off fear and despair. | Always sinister and menacing, even when happy; easily instills fear. Unsettles anyone with less Dark Aura. |
| 75 | Feels the good in others and discerns whether their intentions are good or evil; very hard to fool about intentions. | Greater empathy for what motivates someone's darkest thoughts; knows how to be cruel to each person. |
| 100 | **Immune to nightmare- and fear-based powers.** | **Immune to mental attacks using negative emotions.** |

**50 in both:** a kind of super empathy, an elevated understanding of the personalities around you.

### Healing Expertise

Medical knowledge (instruments, conditions, anatomy, biology, chemistry) and, in the spiritual world, knowledge of how the soul interacts with the body. For this Stat only, **0 is average**: anyone can do basic first aid.

| Healing Expertise | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Basic first aid: bandages, washing wounds. |
| 50 | Properly dresses wounds; stops bleeding, though poorly. |
| 100 | Medical student: lots of knowledge, little practice. |
| 150 | Experienced surgeon. Knows diseases and conditions, and where to cut to make someone bleed out in seconds. |
| 200 | **Green healing aura** from the hands. Heals minor wounds (cuts, scrapes, stab wounds, most flesh wounds) over several posts. Not major injuries. |
| 250 | Draws out poison; heals minor wounds to the soul. |
| 300 | Realigns bones and dislocated joints; can use the aura on themselves. |
| 400 | Heals **major wounds** over a series of posts; self-heals minor wounds and poison. **Arrancar gain High-Speed Regeneration** with 400 before Type boosts. Can inflict crippling damage to almost any part of the body. |
| 600 | **Red anti-healing aura:** reverses healing in progress and reopens recent wounds, including soul wounds, with three touches or ten seconds of contact. **From here, the character's Release must be primarily medical**, used for healing; minor offensive abilities are allowed. |
| 800 | Instantly mends own bones and dislocations; blood filters out poison. Learns healing techniques for the TTP of **one Class lower**. |
| 1,000 | A nearly god-like body and soul: instantly restores minor organ injuries and lost facial features, purges poison from body and soul, fixes bones. |
| 1,200 | Reverses all damage to someone's mind with a touch; restores the insane to sanity. |
