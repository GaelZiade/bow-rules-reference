## Overview

Players are the main characters of B:OW, but they can also create **NPCs**: allies who step into the spotlight and fight alongside their player character (RPC). Outside of DMs, NPCs usually play passive background roles.

An NPC is created in **two steps**: an **NPC application** (with the *NPC Template*) and a **Ryo payment**.

## Creating an NPC

### Creation cost

| NPC Class | 8–5 | 4–3 | 2 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base cost (Ryo) | 5,000 | 10,000 | 15,000 | 30,000 |

| NPC Rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Added cost (Ryo) | — | +2,500 | +7,500 | +15,000 |

### Restrictions

**Class and Rank:**

| Rank | Minimum Class |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Class 4 |
| 3 | Class 2 |
| 4 | Class 1 |

- An NPC can **never exceed the Class or Rank of its associated RPC**. If the RPC is Class 3 and Rank 2, the NPC can never go above either.
- NPCs **can't fill positions an RPC could**: no Fifth Seat or higher for Shinigami. They may have custom positions and Ranks tied to their own plot and their RPC, reviewed during NPC grading.

**Limits:**

- **Two NPCs per Rank** per RPC.
- **No NPC may follow the Path of Hollowfication**, except under the Vizard Leader.
- **No Final Releases.**

### SP

An NPC's total SP is limited by Class, to the **lower** of:

- its race's **training SP cap**, or
- **400 per Class**: 400 at Class 8, 800 at Class 7, and so on.

Example: a Class 6 Shinigami NPC would cap at 1,200 by the 400 rule, but actually caps at **1,000** because of its training cap.

**Exception:** Beast-Clansmen NPCs gain up to **200 training SP per Class**, on top of their normal leveling SP.

### Releases and techniques

- **Releases:** NPCs learn their Release abilities like RPCs. **Restricted Releases** (Bankai, Quinta Etapa) are only available to an RPC who is at least **Class 1 and Rank 3**, and must be earned in a thread like a Class advancement. Alternatively, they can be learned **at NPC creation for double the final price**.
- **Free step technique:** every NPC starts with its race's free step technique **if it qualifies**. A Human NPC must be Class 5 for Bringer Light. Once it qualifies, it gains the technique automatically.
- **Other techniques:** any the NPC's race and Class allow, including custom techniques reviewed by staff, **bought with Ryo**. Class 8 techniques are free; each Class above costs **+1,000 Ryo**: 1,000 for Class 7, 2,000 for Class 6, and so on.
- **Slots:** **15 technique slots.** NPCs use **Amnesia Pills**, like RPCs, to unlearn and relearn techniques.

### Skill Points

NPCs start with **10 SkP**, whatever their Class or Rank. Each extra point costs more than the last:

| Extra SkP | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th | 8th |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost (Ryo) | 5,000 | 10,000 | 15,000 | 20,000 | 25,000 | 30,000 | 35,000 | 40,000 |

**Maximum: 18 SkP.**

## Advancing an NPC

Advancement requires **Ryo** and **proof that the NPC deserves it**.

### Class

Pay for **every individual Class-up**:

| Class-up | 8 → 5 | 5 → 3 | 3 → 2 | 2 → 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per Class (Ryo) | 2,000 | 4,000 | 5,000 | 10,000 |

Each Class-up needs an **explanation and a linked thread**. Paying for several Classes at once still requires justification: a long-term thread showing major character development, or several threads that do.

### Rank

| Rank-up | 1 → 2 | 2 → 3 | 3 → 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost (Ryo) | 10,000 | 30,000 | 50,000 |

Use the **NPC Advancement Template**.

## NPCs in Threads

NPCs can join any thread type: training with their RPC, Socials, Adventures alongside their RPC, or PvPs with the other participants' permission.

### Cost per thread

| Thread | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| **Social, Training** | Free |
| **Adventure, Quest** | By Class and Rank (below) |
| **PvP and other Battles** | **Requires permission** from the other participants; **half** the Adventure cost |

| NPC Class | 8–5 | 4–3 | 2 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adventure/Quest cost (Ryo) | 5,000 | 15,000 | 20,000 | 45,000 |

| NPC Rank | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Added cost (Ryo) | +2,500 | +7,500 | +15,000 |

### Risk

- In **DP or DE** threads, an NPC can be **killed**, and all progress with it is lost. **There is no Ryo refund.**
- NPCs follow the normal rules of fairness and get the same sportsmanship as RPCs.
- **Staff-sponsored threads**, such as events and Canon threads, are **always DP or higher for NPCs** and can kill them.

> **Advice.** Be wary of adding NPCs to threads.

### Ryo from training

An NPC training with you, in a Training or Joint Training thread, earns **50 Ryo for every two paragraphs** completed, in addition to its normal rewards. In Joint Training, every participant, NPCs included, earns 50 Ryo for every two paragraphs written in total. **This does not convert to 25 Ryo per paragraph.**

> **Advice.** It would be smart to post an even number of paragraphs.
