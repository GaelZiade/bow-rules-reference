## Overview

**Ranks** are positions of authority within organizations. Some are prerequisites for higher tiers of Release; others give command over subordinates. Every Rank also maps to a **Type Rank** (1–4), which sets the strength of the character's Type (see *Types*).

### Getting a Rank

- **Open position:** apply for it in the character application, or later with a **CIU**. A CIU promotion needs **at least three threads** justifying it. Any thread type counts, including a lost PvP, as long as it shows the character's desire to improve and grow.
- **Taken position:** **Ranks can be stolen in combat.** If you lose a **Rank Battle**, the challenger takes your Rank and you drop to the next available one. This can cause a chain of battles if the positions beneath you are taken.

### Leader bonuses

Some positions give a **boost to all Stats after Type**. These are marked **+25%** or **+50%** below.

Current holders of each position are listed in the forum's Rankings threads (Soul Society, Hueco Mundo, Human World, Vandenreich, Mercenary).

## Soul Society

The Soul Society has two Rank systems: the **Gotei 13** and the **Beast-Clan packs**.

### Gotei 13 (Shinigami)

Shinigami rise through the seats of their squad up to **Captain**. The very difficult final step is **Captain Commander**, Captain of Squad 1 and head of the whole Gotei 13. Beast-Clansmen who take a Zanpakutō at Class 2 can also hold these Ranks.

**Rogues and Vizards can't hold Shinigami Ranks.** For now, they can only join a Human World organization or become Mercenaries.

### Beast-Clan Packs

Beast-Clansmen outside the Gotei form **packs**, groups under a leader chosen purely by power. Packs work like custom organizations. Members are mostly Beast-Clansmen and like-minded Rogues; others may join. Beast-Clansmen can also be Mercenaries.

**Pack structure:**

- **Four Ranks:** Alpha, Beta, Delta, Omega.
- **Pyramid:** one Alpha, up to three Betas, any number of Deltas and Omegas.
- **Lower Ranks fill first.** A one-member pack only has an Omega. One Omega frees a Delta spot. A Beta needs three lower-Ranked members. An Alpha needs **five** lower-Ranked members, so a pack needs at least **six** members, three of them Deltas or Omegas, to have an Alpha.
- **Membership:** at least **a third** of members must be Beast-Clansmen. The smallest pack able to have an Alpha needs at least two.
- **Class:** each Rank has a Class requirement, and the Rank can't be taken without it even if the pack has enough members.

| Pack Rank | Type Rank | Requirements | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Omega** | 1 | Any Class | Bottom of the pack, at everyone's beck and call: newcomers, or members fallen from grace but worth keeping. |
| **Delta** | 2 | **Class 5+**; at least one Omega | General members; command only the Omegas. |
| **Beta** | 3 | **Class 3+**; at least three lesser members per Beta | Second-in-command; commands most of the pack, always ready to take the Alpha's place. |
| **Alpha** | 4 | **Class 1+**; at least five lesser members; two other members at **Class 2+** | One per pack. Must prove their authority by **fighting and defeating the entire pack at once**. |

**Beast-Clansmen in the Gotei 13:**

| Condition | Ranks available |
| --- | --- |
| Before Class 3 | Unseated Officer |
| Class 3 | Seated Officer up to **Third Seat** |
| With a Zanpakutō (Class 2) | Vice Captain or Captain, meeting the normal requirements (Bankai for Captain, etc.) |
| In the Gotei | Ranked like a Shinigami |

## Hueco Mundo

**Las Noches**, the Arrancar palace, is over 29,000 years old and has had eleven rulers. The tenth, **Azeru Cho (Aizen)**, created the Espada and revitalized Las Noches after it fell into ruin under the eighth ruler. Even after his failed war on the Soul Society, its hierarchy remains, now under a new Shinigami traitor: the **King of Las Noches, Sonken Kouhai** (NPC).

Las Noches holds a reconstructed **Hōgyoku**. It is only a shell of the original, but it can still "unleash the true form of one's power," for example to create Arrancar or Vizards. Kouhai owns it and can use any power his Espada have.

Las Noches has two Rank systems: the **Espada** and the **Exequias**.

Their Leader is on the same tier as the High-Tier Espada.

## Hell

**Satan** (Lucifer), tired of the monotony of torture, now roams the worlds in disguise. He divided Hell into **seven Rings**, one per Deadly Sin: **Pride, Greed, Wrath, Envy, Lust, Gluttony, Sloth**.

- Each Ring is ruled by a **Ringleader**, a High Lord or Lady, who answers only to Satan. Ringleaders come and go from Hell freely and decide whether their underlings may too.
- Each Ring looks the way its Ringleader prefers. Portals in each Ringleader's capitol connect the Rings, and Satan's own Ring is the center.
- The Ringleaders and their underlings are Satan's personal army. Everyone in a Ring is themed around its Sin, in powers or personality. Ringleaders are largely autonomous but should act in Satan's interest.

**Each Ring has:** one **Ringleader**, one **Prince**, three **Marquises**, and unlimited **Knights**.

- **Who can join:** any of the four Hell races, but **Nakabakki must become full Akki** to be a Ringleader.
- **Protection:** position holders are **no longer tortured** while in good standing with their Ringleader. Abuse of this gets them stripped and thrown back into the torture pits.
- **Saika** remain bound by their escape limits, but need no other method of escaping Hell.
- **Pride, Greed, and Wrath** are the most influential Sins in the World of the Living. Their Ringleaders get a **+25%** leader bonus. All Rings are otherwise equal.
- **Contesting:** anyone can be a Ringleader of any Ring, but an occupied Ring must be **contested**. A Ringleader who loses forfeits the bonus and moves to another position.

| Rank | Type Rank | Class | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ringleader** (High Lord/Lady) | 4 | 1+ | Elite overlord of a Ring, usually chosen by Satan. Pride, Greed, Wrath: **+25%**. |
| **Prince** | 3 | 2+ | Serves the Ringleader, manages war strategy, seeks new Sinners who haven't broken their chains, and commands the Marquises and Knights. |
| **Marquis** | 2 | 4+ | Usually takes orders from the Prince. Tracks Nakabakki who haven't joined Hell, and hunts escaped Sinners in the World of the Living. |
| **Knight** | 1 | 8+ | Weak new recruits trained for promotion; attendants to the Prince and Ringleaders. |

### Nakabakki and the Rings

A Nakabakki may join a Ring by swearing loyalty to Satan and **severing all ties** to any former organization, including all organization-specific techniques. Racial Release techniques and other non-organization techniques are kept. **Hell Magic** techniques are only available to Nakabakki who join a Ring.

### Betrayal

**Membership is eternal.** A member who betrays Satan or a Ringleader must make their **very next thread a DE PvP**:

- The opponent is a current member of higher Rank and Class, or **two** members of the same Rank if none is available.
- The thread is **DE for the traitor** and **DP for everyone else**.
- If the traitor loses, they are sent back to Hell and tortured for eternity by one of Satan's Hell Beasts.

**Bounties** are placed on traitors, payable when they are returned to Hell, dead or alive:

| Traitor | Bounty |
| --- | --- |
| Class 8–5 | 5,000 Ryo |
| Class 4–3 | 15,000 Ryo |
| Class 2+ | 30,000 Ryo |
| Any Prince | 50,000 Ryo |
| A Ringleader | 100,000 Ryo |

## Human World

The Human World has several forum-created organizations: **Xcution**, **Uzaki Enterprises**, the **Vandenreich**, the **Sanguine Syndicate**, and the **Substitute Shinigami**. There are also **Mercenaries**.

### Xcution

The largest group of Human World Dwellers. It was once known for corporate business, which was true but only a front, and has returned to the shadows and its roots in **assassination**. It also has a private military for hire, and divisions that control its wealth and research.

**Tattoos.** Every member has a Reiatsu-based **"X" tattoo** marking their division and Rank. Members of the same Rank have it in the same place, and it moves if they change division.

**Personal card.** Every member receives a black card reading *"Welcome to our Xcution,"* linked to their Reiatsu.

- **Summons:** the CEO assigns tasks through it. The card pulses Reiatsu into the member with the message, growing stronger and eventually painful until the member makes clear they will follow, and it gives an instinctive lead to where the CEO wants them.
- **Public contact:** cards a member hands out are linked to that member, so recipients can contact them by following the member's instructions.
- **Key card:** the cards also open Xcution buildings, by Rank:

| Rank | Access |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | Full access |
| 3 | Facilities and rooms assigned to their position and below |
| 2 | Specific materials for their position's development |
| 1 | Must be escorted by Rank 2+ |
| Visitors | Must be escorted by Rank 4 |

| Rank | Type Rank | Tattoo | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Swords** | 1 | Left palm | Orientation-phase members with little authority. At **Class 4** they may take a Rank 2 position in a division; at **Class 1**, a Rank 3 position if one is available, possibly by challenging its holder. Rank 4 positions are earned per their own specifications. |
| **Lances** | 2 | Right palm | Above Swords: part of the private army, or aides to senior officers; may lead small squadrons. Specialists may work for a Rank 3–4 member, such as the Chief of Research, or become a Gun's **Shadow**. |
| **Shadows (of Guns #3–7)** | 2 | Right clavicle | Assassination division (see below). |
| **Shadows (of Guns #1–2)** | 3 | Left clavicle | Assassination division. |
| **Seven Guns #7–3** | 3 | Back of the neck | Assassination division. |
| **Rifles (Seven Guns #1–2)** | 4 | Tongue | Assassination division. |
| **Chief Strategist** | 4 | Left shoulder | Only one. The CEO's right hand and Commander-in-Chief of the private military; directs Xcution's efforts, commands all Swords and Lances, and drafts economic and military policy with the CEO's approval. Demands cunning and intelligence, not raw power. **+25%.** |
| **Chief of Research** | 4 | Right shoulder | Only one. Drives the theories and experiments. Requires a **major scientific project** with great impact on Xcution's goals, developed over **at least three IC threads** of significant research. **+25%.** |
| **CEO** | 4 | — | Held by RPC Shizuka Sanamizu. **+50%.** |

**The assassination division** is separate from the military; some employees don't know it exists.

- **Shadows:**
  - At most **14**, partnered with the Seven Gun of the same number. Each Gun may have **no more than two**.
  - They handle smaller assignments with the efficiency expected of every member.
  - They have no authority over Lances, but a Lance can become a Shadow at a Gun's wish.
  - Their Gun decides how long they keep the position, and may dismiss or **execute** them if they fail.
- **Seven Guns:**
  - Taking an occupied Number requires **trial by combat**, ending in surrender or death. The same applies to moving up a Number.
  - They pull the strings of Xcution's puppets, and may cut them; they handle most recruitment.
  - Each may run their own project with the CEO's approval, but their primary duty is to be the CEO's main stealth and assassination force.

### Uzaki Enterprises (UE)

Outwardly a multifaceted company making everything from phones to weapons, renewable energy, and VR. Its secret **Spiritual Department**, founded and led by CEO **Kazura Uzaki** (RPC), works autonomously from the mortal operations.

- **Headquarters:** a 90-floor skyscraper in Karakura Town's business district, with secret underground floors behind Reiatsu-locks and ID cards. UE also runs an organization-owned shop.
- **Mission:** the betterment and protection of humanity and the Land of the Living's spiritual races, favoring the spiritually unaware and helpless.
- **Influence:** Karakura Town, the rest of Japan, and parts of Russia and Australia.

**Who can join:**

| Races | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Fullbringers, Bounts, Mod Souls, Quincy | No IC requirement |
| Rogues, Beast-Clansmen, Nakabakki, Vizards | Permission from a **Manager** or higher |
| Arrancar | Permission from a **Board Member** or the **CEO** |
| Hollows, Akki, Saika | Considered enemies unless proven exceptions |
| Shinigami | Allies, but can't be hired directly |

**Promotion** works like a corporate ladder, by application or invitation:

- A **Manager** may promote any Junior Employee to Senior Employee.
- A **Board Member** may promote any Senior Employee to Manager of their own sub-department.

**Salary** is paid in Ryo per IC report, per thread:

| Rank | Type Rank | Requirements | PvP / Joint Training | Adventure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Junior Employee** | 1 | Any Class | 500 | 2,000 |
| **Senior Employee** | 2 | **Class 4+**; Manager's recommendation or application | 1,000 | 4,000 |
| **Manager** | 3 | **Class 2+**; invitation or application, approved by the CEO or a Board Member | 2,000 | 6,000 |
| **Board Member** | 4 | **Class 1+**; exceptional trust and value; invitation or a detailed application | 2,000 | 6,000 |
| **CEO** | 4 | Held by RPC Kazura Uzaki | — | — |

- **Junior Employees:** spiritually aware employees who haven't manifested their powers or aren't ready for solo missions.
- **Senior Employees:** have manifested their basic or developing powers and can work solo, in the field or developing items.
- **Managers:** oversee Juniors and Seniors in a sub-department of their own creation (Security, Operations, R&D…). A Senior Employee may **contest** a Manager to lead the sub-department better; a Board Member or the CEO decides whether leadership changes, transferring or demoting the old Manager.
- **Board Members:** shape policy with the CEO, process applications, and decide case-by-case hires of races that would traditionally oppose UE's mission. Usually not contested, and they oversee no single sub-department. **Top 3 Board Members: +25%.**
- **CEO:** **+50%.**

**Vote of No Confidence** against a Board Member:

- One Board Member alone can prompt an investigation for removal.
- 2 Managers = 1 Board Member, and 3 Senior Employees = 1 Manager.
- Junior Employees can't vote.
- At least one Manager must take part.

**Shop items:** employees of any Rank may design items for the company shop. They are approved by a Manager or higher and produced with company funds. The creator earns a **10% commission** on sales, negotiable case by case.

### The Vandenreich

An empire of Quincy, the **"Invisible Empire,"** with thousands of members, because it can manifest Quincy powers in other races. Free Quincy only learned their powers; the Vandenreich can imbue them into **Fullbringers, Shinigami, Arrancar, and Bounts**, the only races that can be converted (see *Races*).

- **Base:** **Silbern**, a massive ice-covered palace secret to outsiders.
- **Ice Castles:** units led by a **Captain or higher** can raise a temporary base anywhere. The surrounding climate becomes an icy wasteland while it stands.
- **Goal:** to annihilate the Soul Society. Lesser goals include usurping the Soul King's power and role as God, and purging all non-Quincy and non-Human races.
- **Leader:** the **Emperor**. No one knows his true name or identity; he is simply the one they worship.

**Sternritter** is a **title**, not a Rank, so holders keep their original Rank.

- **Who:** at most **26**, one per letter of the alphabet. Five are the Generals; the rest are Captain Supervisors and Captains.
- **Name:** each receives a new name and an aspect matching their letter (e.g. "Paiva, P, The Particle"). Powers usually, but not always, relate to it.
- **Benefits:** the Emperor's favor. A Sternritter can **take a stolen Bankai** from any non-Sternritter member, and can **instantly negate the powers** of inferior-Ranked members.
- **Role:** chosen for destructive ability and authority; they are the main offensive line. Most members call them by their Sternritter names, while Sternritter use each other's real names.
- **Letters:** "A" is always the Emperor; the other letters don't reflect strength. The current Sternritter were recently purged and replaced, and are considered "purified."
- **Earning it:** you can't simply apply. A member of high enough Rank demonstrates loyalty and battlefield competence by submitting a **Rank Update** with links to threads showing their service.

It has no wish to conquer the Three Worlds, only to keep the status quo and its members rich and out of the legal light.

### Substitute Shinigami

Not Shinigami at all: **Fullbringers** certified by the Gotei 13 as Human World contacts and liaisons with other Human World organizations.

**Becoming one:** a human asks any Shinigami they meet, if they know the Rank exists, and the Shinigami reports it to the Gotei 13. Alternatively, a Shinigami invites the human.

**Rights:** they may legally slay Hollows for the Gotei. They may also execute members of races deemed a threat to the Soul Society or Human World: mainly Hollows and Arrancar, but this can extend to the Vandenreich and Bounts. Current policy lets them work with unaffiliated Quincy, who are not legal to slay.

**The Shinigami Representative Badge** proves their Rank to any Shinigami.

- It can perform **Konsō** (Soul Burial) in place of a Zanpakutō.
- **Inseparable:** it can't be abandoned, stolen, or destroyed. If destroyed, it reforms; if lost, it returns to the owner's pocket.
- **Tracking:** the Gotei can track the owner at any time while the badge is their property.
- **Containment:** it gathers the owner's Reiatsu, which lets a **Vice Captain or higher** subjugate and neutralize them. A **Captain** can make the owner **self-destruct and die**.

| Rank | Type Rank | Requirements | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shinigami Representative** | 2 | **Class 4+**, physical age **20+**, a personality evaluation | — |
| **Representative Lieutenant** | 3 | **Class 1+**, physical age **35+**, a leadership merit personality test | Only **two**. Authority over Seated Officers and lower Shinigami, and over other Substitutes. A Vice Captain or higher out-ranks them. |

### Mercenaries

Freelancers who use their powers as they please.

- **Who:** Human World Dwellers (including Rogues and Vizards), Saika, and Beast-Clansmen.
- **Ranks:** 1 to 4. A new Mercenary starts at **Rank 1**, unless they test higher or have major previous accomplishments.
- **Advancement:** based on **influence and success**: mostly plot-based accomplishments and **kill count**, the number of NPC and RPC kills.
- **To request or test** for the next Rank, private-message a staff member.

## Custom Organizations

Members can create their own organizations, with a specific purpose: research, defense, criminal enterprise, religion, and so on. **Shinigami cannot create custom organizations.** To create one, see *Character Creation*.

| Classification | Minimum members | Rank positions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | **3**, from at least 2 players | Rank 1 or 2. Rank 2 spots are granted by merit. |
| **Growing** | **5**, from at least 2 players\* | As a guideline, **1 Rank 3 per 2 RPC members**. |
| **Established** | **7**, from at least 2 players\* | Rank 3 as Growing, plus **1 Rank 4 per 4 RPC members**. |
| **Legacy** | **9**, from at least **3** players\* | Rank 3 and 4 as before. With 9+ members, **one Rank 4 leadership position** that guides the organization gets **+25%**. A **mechanical perk** for members, approved by staff. |

\*The member requirement may be waived for groups with exceptional impact on the site. Position guidelines may be waived with enough merit from the applicants and the organization.

- Rank names inside the organization can be anything.
- **Promotions:** members apply through a normal **Rank Update**, based on their own and the organization's merit.
- **Everyone starts as New.** To upgrade, the Leader or one of the highest-Ranked members applies with:
  - the list of active members
  - notable individual and organizational achievements
  - the plans for the future

  Staff decide whether to upgrade and how many Rank 3/4 positions to grant. Rejections always include a reason and suggestions.

**Limits at all times:**

- Never **more Rank 3/4 members than Rank 1/2 members**.
- **A majority of members must be RPCs.**
- A player may have any number of characters in an organization, but for size requirements **each player counts as one member**.
