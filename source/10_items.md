## Overview

**Items** are tools that help characters in threads: basic weapons, crafted equipment, and accessories with specific purposes. Many are on par with basic techniques, and some have unique effects that can change the flow of battle.

## Categories

| Category | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapons** | Tools designed to directly harm opponents or their belongings, from melee weapons like swords and spears to consumables like explosives. |
| **Armor** | Protection or covering for the body: damage reduction, concealment, shields that block damage outright. |
| **Tools** | Combat items that fit no other category, such as wires or tools used as the basis of techniques (e.g. Gintō tubes). |
| **Utility** | General items often not used in battle, either because they have no combat use or because they work equally well outside it (e.g. the Soul Gauge). |

Some items sit in unexpected categories to keep paired items together, such as ammunition under firearms instead of consumables.

## Reading an Item

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The item's name. |
| **Description** | What it looks like and what it's for. |
| **Item Type** | Weapon, Armor, Tool (combat), or Utility (non-combat). |
| **Class** | The range of Classes the item covers. A sword might be Class 8–1, since it can be made sturdier with different materials and techniques; a basic smoke grenade might only be Class 8–6, with higher Classes giving it a wider range. **You must be of equal or higher Class than an item to buy it**, whatever your money. |
| **Item Power (IP)** | A static value used **instead of the user's OS** for the item's offense or defense, and in clashes with other abilities. Written as *base + gain per Class*. |
| **Offense Type** | **Physical** or **Spiritual**, which decides what can defend against it. A basic sword is Physical, stopped by physical barriers and flex defense. A gun firing Bala-like bullets is Spiritual, so only spiritual flex works. Extra damage types go here too (e.g. Spiritual/Fire, so fire-affecting techniques apply). |
| **Durability** | How much punishment the item takes (see below). |
| **Bonuses** | Non-damaging properties. Example: spend 100 Reiatsu so the next attack is Spiritual instead of Physical and bypasses a high-Strength flex defense; or a smoke bomb's blinding effect, range, and the senses needed to see through it. |
| **Drawbacks** | Balance bonuses and IP, as with techniques: a greatsword attacks a bit slower, a cursed item drains Reiatsu every post. |
| **Cost** | Written as *base cost + increase per Class*. |

### Item Power

**Example:** an item of Class 7–4 with IP **100 + 75/Class** has:

| Class | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IP | 100 | 175 | 250 | 325 |

If damage exceeds the IP of a defensive item or armor, **the remaining force carries through** to the blocker.

Cost scales the same way: at **1,000 + 750/Class**, the item costs 1,000 at its base Class, 1,750 one Class up, 2,500 two Classes up, and so on.

### Durability

- **Normal durability:** **3× base IP** for defensive items; **2× base IP** for other non-consumables. Exceptions can be a bonus, traded for drawbacks, or a drawback, a frailer item that gets more bonuses.
- **Consumables:** durability equal to their IP, since they're meant to be destroyed on use.

When an attack **exceeds the item's IP**, the item takes damage depending on the Class gap:

| Attack's Class vs. item's | Damage to durability |
| --- | --- |
| Equal, or within one Class | **50%** of the attack's OS |
| **Two Classes higher** | **100%** of the attack's OS |
| **Two Classes lower** | **None**: the craftsmanship holds against insufficient attacks |

The forum's text says "two Classes greater" for both of the last two cases, but its example (below) shows the no-damage case applies to attacks two Classes **lower**.

**Example.** A sword with **600 IP** has **1,200 durability**.

- A 700-OS attack does 350 damage. Four hits deal 1,400 and break it.
- If the sword is Class 4 and the attack is **Class 2 or higher**, **two hits** break it.
- Attacks from **Class 6 or lower** can't damage it at all.

### Broken items

| Thread | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **DP or DE** | A broken item is **lost permanently**. |
| **NDE** | The item gets a **strike**, recorded in the Point Bank's item list. **Three strikes** break it permanently, even in NDE. |
| Any | **Consumables** are always lost on use. |

## Item Prices

A **guide** to maximum value by Class, not a fixed rule. Many items cost less, and some more, depending on effects and stats.

| Item Class | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Max. guide price (Ryo) | 3,000 | 4,500 | 6,000 | 9,000 | 12,000 | 15,000 | 50,000 |

Prices are tailored to Rank budgets:

- **Class 7–4** items fit a **Rank 1** budget.
- **Class 3–2** items fit **Rank 2**.
- **Class 1** items fit **Rank 3+**.

Lower Ranks can still buy higher items.

## Creating Items

Every item uses the **Custom Item Template**.

### For Urahara's Shop

There are no requirements except that the item suits **Urahara's Shop**. The idea is pitched to Urahara, who covers the skills and costs of production.

- These are generally **practical combat items**, plus smaller functional tools. No large-scale lethal tools, such as toxic gases or explosives that can demolish large regions.
- Moderators decide whether an item goes into the shop. An item that is **approved but declined** for Urahara's Shop is still eligible for a custom shop, if other requirements are met.

### For a custom shop or personal use

The player builds the item, so the character needs both the **Skill** and the **funds**.

**Skill required.** A Skill related to the item (e.g. *Weapon: Swords* to craft standard swords, and only those), or SkP in **Crafting**.

| Item Class | 7–5 | 4–2 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skill level | 1 | 2 | 3 |

**Ryo required:**

| Item | Cost to the creator |
| --- | --- |
| **Shop item** | **50%** of the finished item's cost, **once**, paid when staff fully approve it. |
| **Reserved item** (only for the creator; not sold) | **100%** of the base price, paid **every time** one is made (consumables, multiple copies). This encourages creating for everyone, not just yourself. |
| **Commissioned item** (made for another player) | The buyer pays **100%** of the base price for **one copy**, which goes to the buyer, not the crafter. The crafter receives **50%** of what was paid; the other 50% is lost. |

## Shop Threads

Players may open shops, alongside Urahara's Shop, to sell items their character can actually make.

- **Requirement:** at least **Class 3**.
- **Request:** describe the shop in a thread and submit it for approval. At the top, note that it **must be approved by STAFF only, not Senior Graders**.
- **Items:** use the Custom Item Template for every item.
- **The thread:** once approved, open the shop thread in the board where it sells (any location board). Staff will sticky it.
- **Proceeds** go straight to the shop's owner and must be noted in the Point Bank after every purchase.
