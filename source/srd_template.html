Unofficial. This reference was written from the forum's rules threads (Getting Started, Forum Mechanics, Miscellaneous Rules, and the Quick Reference). The rules are restated more directly, but no rule was added. Each chapter links to its source threads. If anything here differs from the forum, the forum is authoritative.

About This Reference

This book restates the rules threads of Bleach: Online World, found in the boards Introduction: Getting Started, Forum Mechanics: Character Creation, Forum Mechanics: Roleplaying, and Other Mechanics: Miscellaneous Rules, plus the Quick Reference page. They are reorganized for clarity, with tables and short sections.

Written from the forum's text. Every rule here comes from the original threads; nothing was added. Every number in the source threads was checked against this book. Where the forum contradicts itself or has an obvious typo, a note in italics says so.

Rules and advice. The main text states rules and concepts. Guidance on building characters and roleplaying is set apart in Advice boxes.

Left out on purpose: the application passwords (find them by reading the rules on the forum) and personal first-person asides from staff.

The forum is authoritative. Rules change. If anything here differs from the live forum, the forum wins. Each chapter lists its source threads.

All rules are the work of the Bleach: Online World staff and members. This is an unofficial reading copy for the community, not affiliated with Bleach or its rights holders.
